Economy upgrades make your existing villagers gather faster. Unlike a new villager, they cost **no Town Center time** and no population slot — so they never compete with continuous villager production. That is why the golden rule is simple: **never skip your eco upgrades**. A few hundred resources spent now compounds into thousands gathered later.

## Why they are so strong

A research costs resources once, but every villager affected gathers faster for the rest of the game. **Double-Bit Axe**, the first Lumber Camp upgrade, speeds up every woodcutter. With around **10 villagers on wood, it is worth roughly 2 extra villagers** of wood income — for a fraction of the cost and zero TC time. That is the kind of return that wins games.

## Lumber Camp upgrades — top priority

Wood feeds almost everything in the early game: farms, houses, buildings, and ranged units. The Lumber Camp line (**Double-Bit Axe → Bow Saw → Two-Man Saw**) is the **highest-priority eco upgrade set** in the game. Research Double-Bit Axe as soon as you can afford it in Feudal Age, and keep the line going as you age up.

## Farm upgrades — wood-saving, not speed

The Mill upgrades — **Horse Collar, Heavy Plow, and Crop Rotation** — do not make farmers gather faster. Instead, each one increases how much **food a single farm holds before it runs out**. More food per farm means **fewer refreshes**, and every farm refresh costs **60 wood**.

So farm upgrades are really **wood-saving** upgrades. They matter more the more farms you have, which is why they are **often delayed** until your farm count is high or your wood is comfortable.

## Wheelbarrow and Hand Cart

These two are researched at the **Town Center** and speed up villager movement and carrying capacity.

| Upgrade | Where | Cost | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wheelbarrow | Town Center | ~75s research (≈ 3 villagers of time) | Around 14–16 farms |
| Hand Cart | Town Center | 500 resources | Usually delayed to Imperial |

**Wheelbarrow** takes about 75 seconds to research — that is roughly the time it takes to make **3 villagers** — so do not click it the instant it unlocks. It pays off once you have around **14–16 farms**, when faster walking between farm and drop-off saves real time.

**Hand Cart** is the bigger version. At **500 resources** it is expensive, so it is **usually delayed until Imperial Age** when your economy can absorb the cost.

## Mining Camp upgrades

Gold and stone mining upgrades (**Gold Mining, Stone Mining**, then the Shaft Mining tier) follow the same logic as wood, but you usually have fewer villagers on those resources. They are commonly **left until you are aging up** anyway, so research them while you wait for the next age to complete and you lose nothing.

## The takeaway

Eco upgrades are free in the only currency that matters early — TC time. Prioritize **Lumber Camp first**, fold in **farm upgrades** as your farm count grows, time **Wheelbarrow** around 14–16 farms, and pick up mining and **Hand Cart** as your economy can afford them.
