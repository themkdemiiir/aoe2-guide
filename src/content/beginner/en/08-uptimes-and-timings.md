Your **uptime** is the in-game clock when you click up to the next age. It is not a vibe — it is arithmetic. Because every villager and the **Loom** each take exactly **25 seconds** to produce, your Feudal uptime is a direct function of how many things you queue before clicking up.

## Feudal uptime is just pop math

Add up everything the Town Center makes before you age up, multiply by 25 seconds, and you have a hard floor on your uptime.

| Plan | What you make | Realistic uptime |
|---|---|---|
| 22 pop (lean) | 21 vils + scout + Loom | ~10:05 |
| 23 pop + Loom | 22 vils + scout + Loom | ~10:30 |
| 18 pop (fast) | fewer vils, no Loom | ~8:25 |

Lower pop means a faster, more aggressive uptime but a thinner economy. Higher pop means a slower clock but more villagers waiting in Feudal. Neither is "correct" — they are different gameplans. What matters is that you **know your number** and hit it consistently.

## Why clean timings win games

A timing is your army arriving the moment your opponent is most vulnerable. Hit a sharp one and you can cripple an enemy economy before they can react:

- **3 Men-at-Arms** at the right minute force villagers off resources and into the Town Center.
- **3–4 Fletching archers** out-range Spearmen and pick off gatherers from safety.
- **3 scouts** snipe stragglers and deny boar lures.

The damage is not just the kills — it is every second of villager **idle time** you inflict. A scout pinning six villagers under the TC is worth far more than the one villager it actually kills.

## Pushing into Castle Age

The same discipline carries into your Castle Age transition. To keep the clock clean:

- **Keep wood low.** Banked wood is unspent economy. Float only what your next buildings need.
- **Seed farms early** so food keeps flowing while villagers shift jobs.
- **Move to gold at the right time** — too early starves your military, too late stalls the age-up.
- **Pre-build your age-up buildings** (Blacksmith, extra production) before you click, so you are spending the instant you arrive.

## Castle and Imperial benchmarks

Use these to measure yourself against the meta:

| Target | Good | Fastest (strong civ) |
|---|---|---|
| **3 TC arbalest** | under ~32:00 | 26–28 min with strong archer civs |
| **Scout / Knight Imperial** | ~32–36 min | — |

If your real games land a few minutes behind these, the leak is almost always **idle production** or **floating resources** — not a missing trick. Fix the boring stuff first.
