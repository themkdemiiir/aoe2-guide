# Fast Castle into Unique Unit

**Goal:** Reach Castle Age quickly at 29 population, immediately build a Castle, and begin producing Unique Units for an aggressive Castle Age push.
**Recommended civs:** Spanish, Turks, Mayans, Burgundians, Mongols

## Dark Age (0–27 Villagers)

- 6 villagers on sheep (build 2 houses with the first villagers, then send them to sheep)
- 4 villagers on wood; the first builds a Lumber Camp
- 1 villager lures the first boar
- 2 villagers build 2 houses near the berries, then a Mill on the berries
- +3 villagers to berries
- +2 villagers build farms
- +3 villagers to wood (build a 2nd Lumber Camp at ~villager 19), adding houses as needed
- 1 villager builds a Gold Mining Camp and mines gold (~villager 24)
- 1 villager builds a Stone Mining Camp and mines stone (~villager 26)
- +2 villagers to stone, and pull 2 villagers off the depleting sheep to stone

Click Feudal Age with the 27th villager (~12:30). As the sheep run out, send those villagers to farms.

## Feudal Preparation

- 1 wood villager builds a Blacksmith
- 2 wood villagers build a Market
- +2 villagers continue to stone (28–29) to bank enough for the Castle

**Research:**
- Double-Bit Axe
- Horse Collar

## Feudal Age (27 Villagers)

- Click Castle Age (~15:30) — minimize time in Feudal
- Keep stockpiling stone so the Castle can go up the instant you reach Castle Age
- When berry villagers free up, redirect to wood, gold, or farms as needed

## Castle Age (29 Villagers)

- Take 5–7 villagers to build the Castle immediately
- Begin producing multiple Unique Units from the Castle
- Research Bow Saw
- Continue producing villagers; redirect Castle builders back to the economy once it is up

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- A Castle + Unique Unit pressure shortly after ~15:30 is very difficult to answer without a Castle of your own.
- **Weak against:** Opponent building their own Castle before yours; aggressive Feudal attacks that disrupt the stone economy.
- **Strong against:** Opponents going for a slow boom; Fast Castle opponents without their own Unique Unit ready.

**Options:**

1. **Safe Development:** Begin Unique Unit production, then slowly add Town Centers; send 2–3 Castle builders back to mine and gather.
2. **Aggressive Castle Drop:** Keep gathering stone; continuously produce Unique Units; drop a second Castle near the enemy within 2–3 minutes of Castle Age.
3. **Single TC Pressure:** After the Castle is finished, return to a wood/gold/farm balance; apply sustained pressure with Unique Units + Siege from one Town Center.

**Defense Tip:**
Make sure your Castle is always earning value — if it is not defending or attacking, you are falling behind economically. No matter which path you choose, the Castle must justify its 650 stone cost through combat value or opponent disruption.
