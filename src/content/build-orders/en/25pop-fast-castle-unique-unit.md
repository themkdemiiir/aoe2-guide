# Fast Castle into Unique Unit

Rush to Castle Age at 25 pop, immediately drop a Castle, and flood Unique Units while the opponent is still scrambling for an answer.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- The payoff is timing: a Castle plus a stream of Unique Units around the 15-minute mark hits before most opponents have a Castle of their own to trade with.
- This build wants a civ whose un-upgraded Unique Unit is already strong off the production building — Conquistadors, Janissaries, Plumed Archers, Coustilliers, or Mangudai all spike hard the moment the Castle is up.
- Bank stone aggressively in Feudal. The whole plan collapses if you reach Castle Age and cannot afford the Castle right away, so protect those stone miners.
- Once the first Castle is producing, decide between a forward Castle to apply pressure or a defensive one at home that lets you add Town Centers behind it.
- You are light on military until the Castle finishes, so wall your stone and gold and keep the scout alive to spot any early Feudal aggression.
- If the opponent also Fast Castles, your edge is the Unique Unit itself — out-produce them and force fights on your terms rather than booming passively.
