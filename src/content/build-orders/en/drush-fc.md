# Drush Fast Castle

Harass with three Dark Age Militia, then reach Castle Age first with a clean economic and technology lead.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- The Drush is the cheap part: three Militia plus the Scout poke the opponent's eco while you commit fully to the climb. Even one or two villager kills forces them off resources, and the panic alone slows their own up-time.
- This is a true Drush, not a Men-at-Arms rush — never spend on the MAA upgrade. Every spare resource feeds the Fast Castle, so you land Castle Age around 16:05 with the lead intact.
- Wall as you go. Palisades around the boar and woodlines keep your Militia builders safe and stop the opponent from trading the harassment back onto your own villagers.
- Out of Castle Age, mass Archers from your two Ranges and follow Double-Bit Axe, Horse Collar, and Fletching to snowball the eco-and-tech advantage into a Crossbow timing.
- Strong civs here are the archer powers — Britons, Ethiopians, Italians, Mayans, and Vikings — whose cheaper or harder-hitting Archers convert the Castle-first lead directly into a winning unit.
- If the opponent is already walled and turtling, drop the harassment early and lean on the boom: an early second Town Center behind your walls pulls the eco lead further ahead.
