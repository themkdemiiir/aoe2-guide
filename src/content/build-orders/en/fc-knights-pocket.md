# Fast Castle 2-Stable Knights (Pocket)

**Goal:** Boom to ~28–30 villagers, arrive in Castle Age, then flood Knights from 2 Stables to support your team's flanks.
**Recommended civs:** Franks (Chivalry extra HP), Lithuanians (relics boost attack), Berbers (faster cavalry), Huns (no houses), Magyars (free Husbandry)

## Dark Age (0–23 Villagers)

- 6 villagers on sheep
- 3 villagers on wood; Lumber Camp
- 3 villagers to berries; Mill
- 3 more villagers to wood (15 total)
- 8 more villagers to food/wood split (23 total)
- Research Loom

Click Feudal Age at 22–23 population (~10:00–10:30).

## Feudal Preparation

- Build Blacksmith + Market
- 4 villagers to gold; Mining Camp
- Continue TC production to 28–30 villagers
- Build Farms; research Double-Bit Axe

Click Castle Age at ~28 population (~14:00).

## Castle Age (28 Villagers)

- Build 2 Stables simultaneously
- Research Bloodlines (HP upgrade for Knights)
- Produce Knights non-stop from both Stables
- Research Forging + Scale Barding Armor
- Add 2 more gold villagers as queue grows
- Send Knights to support flanks or raid opponent pockets

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

The Pocket Knight rush is the dominant team game strategy. In 4v4 pocket position, your flanks protect you — use that safety to boom aggressively and output maximum Knights.

- **Weak against:** Spearman + Archer combinations; Camel civs (Saracens, Hindustanis, Gurjaras) hard-counter Knights.
- **Strong against:** Any army without Spearmen or Camels; Crossbow-heavy flanks that need cavalry support.

**Options:**

1. **Trebs + Knights:** Add Trebs to siege TC while Knights raid.
2. **Paladins:** Research in Imperial Age for a massive power spike.
3. **Raid Support:** Send 3–4 Knights to each allied flank to kill Mangonels threatening your allies.
