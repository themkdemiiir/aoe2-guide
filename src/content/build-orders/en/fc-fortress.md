# Fast Castle on Fortress

Reach Castle Age around 10:40 from the Fortress map's pre-built start, using its free walls and towers as cover while your eco snowballs.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- The Fortress map hands you a ready-made base — 10 villagers, a scout, five farms, a barracks, a castle, four houses, four towers, and stone walls — so you start safe and never spend wood on defenses. Lean on that to commit fully to the economy.
- Keep the boar and deer flowing into the Town Center: tight food management is what lets this build hit Feudal near 7:10 with only seven villagers on wood.
- Feudal is just a transit age. Drop a Market and Blacksmith with three wood villagers, heal your scout, and click Castle immediately — Double-Bit Axe and Horse Collar go down the moment you arrive.
- As berries deplete in Castle Age, walk those villagers straight to your first extra Town Center site so your boom never stalls.
- Use the free castle and towers to deter raids while you out-eco the opponent; you are trading early aggression for a lead you must convert in Castle Age.
- From here, lean into your civ's Castle-Age power spike — knights for Franks or Persians, monks and relics behind the walls, or extra Town Centers into Imperial if the game goes long.
