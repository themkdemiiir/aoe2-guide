# 21 Vils Malay Flexible Opening

Reach Feudal a step ahead of the field on Malay's faster age-up, then commit to Archers, Scouts, or Spear-Skirm only after you read what the opponent is doing.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- Malay's cheaper, faster age-up is the whole point: the same villager flow lands Feudal around 9:13 while leaving you roughly two villagers ahead of a standard opener, and that surplus is what funds the early Barracks and the follow-up of your choice.
- Keep your options open through the early Barracks — it is built before you have decided anything, so whichever fork you pick is producing the moment you spot the enemy's plan.
- Fork A (Archers): drop a single Range and shift four villagers to gold for a clean Archer line; this is the default answer when the opponent leans on infantry or stays passive.
- Fork B (Scouts): a Stable and Scout Cavalry punish greedy economies and unwalled flanks, buying time to transition once the harass has done its work.
- Fork C (Spear-Skirm): Malay's free infantry armor makes Spearman-plus-Skirmisher unusually durable, a cheap gold-light wall against enemy Archers and Scouts.
- Whichever branch you take, the +2 villager edge compounds: farms in behind a fast Blacksmith and Fletching let you spike into a faster Castle or Imperial Age than your opponent can match.
