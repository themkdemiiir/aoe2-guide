# Romans 5 Man-at-Arms Rush

Reach Feudal Age around 9:15 with five Militia ready, then convert them to Man-at-Arms and lean on the Roman infantry armor bonus to grind down the opponent.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- The whole opener is built around five Man-at-Arms backed by the Roman +2 infantry armor — that extra armor makes each unit far more resilient, so your group trades up against archers, Scouts, and other early infantry.
- Get the Militia out early and keep all five alive; the damage they do to undefended Villagers and Houses is what buys time for your slightly slower economy to catch up.
- Pair the Man-at-Arms with your Scout so you can chase down stragglers and pin the enemy in their base while you finish the Blacksmith upgrades.
- React to what they build: add an Archery Range with Skirmishers if they go Archers, and keep walling and dropping farms behind the aggression so you do not stall.
- This opening stays flexible into Castle Age — fold the farms into a fast Castle behind a Market and wall, then continue with Long Swordsmen and Scorpions, switch to Knights, or simply boom and grab Relics if the rush has bought you the lead.
