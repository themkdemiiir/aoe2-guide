# 30 Pop Fast Imperial Age (Generic)

**Goal:** Reach Imperial Age at ~21 minutes through a balanced economy with no Feudal military investment.
**Recommended civs:** Any civ with strong Imperial Age units

## Dark Age (0–30 Villagers)

- 6 villagers on sheep
- 10 villagers on wood (2 Lumber Camps)
- 4 villagers on berries (Mill)
- 8 villagers on boar, deer, or sheep
- 2 villagers on gold
- When hunting runs out, transition villagers to Farms
- Use scout to push deer toward TC

Click Feudal Age with 30th villager (~13:00).

## Feudal Preparation

- 1 villager builds Blacksmith
- 2 villagers build Market (return to wood when done)
- +2 villagers to gold

**Research:**
- (Delay) Double-Bit Axe and Horse Collar in Feudal if time permits

## Feudal Age (30 Villagers)

- Click Castle Age (~16:30) — minimize Feudal time
- No military production in Feudal Age
- Use Market to sell surplus resources for gold if needed

## Castle Age (32 Villagers)

- Build 1 Monastery
- Build 1 Siege Workshop
- +2 villagers to wood
- When berries run out, send villagers to Farms
- Keep economy balanced; increase gold count
- Click Imperial Age (~21:00)

## Imperial Age (34 Villagers)

- Target resource distribution: 12 food, 9 wood, 8 gold
- Build military as you transition to Imperial Age (Archers, Artillery, Monks)
- Expand economy and multiply production buildings
- Add military production buildings to match chosen composition

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- The 30 Pop Fast Imp is a long-term investment strategy. It works best on closed maps where Feudal pressure cannot disrupt the passive economy.
- **Weak against:** Feudal or Castle Age aggression that reaches your base before walling is complete; any opponent who can apply sustained pressure.
- **Strong against:** Boom strategies that reach Imperial Age at the same time but with a weaker military; opponents who invest heavily in Castle Age units.

**Options:**

1. **Trebuchet + Paladin:** Standard Imperial Age composition for civs with strong cavalry and siege.
2. **Bombard Cannon + Infantry:** Effective for breaking down castle defenses; works for any civ with access to Bombard Cannons.
3. **Monks + Siege:** Add Monks for conversion support alongside Mangonels/Trebuchets for a cost-effective all-in push.

**Defense Tip:**
Seal all walls before Feudal Age ends. Build a few Spearmen at minimum in case Scouts pressure your economy early. Once Imperial Age lands, commit fully to a composition — hesitation at Imperial Age costs more than committing to the wrong units.
