# Fast Imperial Age (Generic)

**Goal:** Balanced economy leading to a fast Imperial Age transition.

**Target Timings:**
- Feudal → ~13:00
- Castle → ~16:30
- Imperial → ~21:00

## Dark Age (0–30 Villagers)

- 6 Sheep
- 10 Wood (2 Lumber Camps)
- 4 Berries (Mill)
- 8 Boar / Deer / Sheep
- 2 Gold

**When hunting runs out:**
- Transition villagers to Farms.
- Use scout to push deer toward the TC.

Click Feudal Age with 30th villager.

## Feudal Age (30–32 Villagers)

- 1 Blacksmith
- 2 Market
  - After building, return these villagers to wood.
- +2 Gold

Click Castle Age (~16:30)

## Castle Age (32–34 Villagers)

- 1 Monastery
- 1 Siege Workshop
- +2 Wood

When berries run out, send villagers to Farms.
Keep economy balanced; increase gold count.

## Imperial Age (34+)

**Economy distribution (approximate):**
- 12 Food (Farms)
- 9 Wood
- 8 Gold

Build military as you transition to Imperial (archers, artillery, monks).
Expand economy and multiply production buildings.
