# Double Stable Scouts

Land in Feudal at 18 pop around 8:50, then ramp into two stables so a steady stream of Scout Cavalry overwhelms the opponent's economy.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- This is the higher-commitment cousin of the single-stable opening: the second stable doubles your scout output, so the build only pays off if you keep both queues full and your TC busy at the same time. Floating resources here means you bought the extra stable for nothing.
- Use the first wave of scouts to find the enemy economy and harass exposed villagers, then time the second stable to land just as your farm transition gives you the food to sustain double production.
- Bloodlines and Blacksmith upgrades are the spikes that win these fights — fold them in once 7–8 scouts are already out so you never stall military for tech.
- Read your scout before adding spears: against an enemy scout opening, a handful of Spearmen plus your own scouts win the trade, but against archers skip them and lean on numbers and walls instead.
- Civ notes: Franks lack Bloodlines, so lean on Husbandry for speed and raw numbers; Huns skip houses entirely, freeing wood to start the stables earlier; Magyars get cheaper scouts and a free attack upgrade that makes the mass hit harder.
- Default Castle Age follow-up is a smooth transition into Knights from the same two stables; keep the gold flowing and add a third TC or more farms once the raiding has bought you map control.
