# Fast Castle into Boom

**Goal:** Reach Castle Age as quickly as possible, then immediately deploy additional Town Centers to multiply villager production.
**Recommended civs:** Chinese, Britons, Franks, Teutons, Vikings

## Dark Age (0–26 Villagers)

- 6 villagers on sheep (vils 4–6 join the starting three)
- 4 villagers on wood (vil 7 builds the lumber camp, then all gather wood)
- 1 villager lures the first boar (vil 11)
- 5 villagers on berries — build 2 houses and a Mill next to the berries first, then gather (vils 12–16)
- Re-task one of the boar villagers to lure the second boar
- 2 villagers seed farms next to the TC or Mill (vils 17–18)
- 5 villagers on wood — build a 2nd lumber camp and a house, then gather wood (vils 19–23)
- 3 villagers on gold — build the gold mining camp, then mine (vils 24–26)

Click Feudal Age at 26 population.

## Feudal Preparation

- Take 1 villager from wood to build the Blacksmith
- Take 2 villagers from wood to build the Market
- Sell excess stone at the Market if short on food or gold
- No military buildings in Feudal Age

**Research:**

- Research Double-Bit Axe in the Lumber Camp during Feudal Age

## Feudal Age (26 Villagers)

- Click Castle Age as soon as the Blacksmith and Market are placed
- No military production in Feudal Age
- Pour every resource into the age-up
- Buy 100 stone at the Market if you intend to build a fourth Town Center

## Castle Age (28 Villagers)

- Immediately build 2 additional Town Centers next to resources, pulling 4 villagers from each woodline
- An optional fourth Town Center requires purchasing 100 stone via the Market
- Research Horse Collar in the Mill to boost farming
- Transition sheep villagers onto Farms as the sheep deplete
- By 20 minutes you should have 40+ villagers producing continuously

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- Fast Castle Boom is the canonical defensive/economic strategy. The entire early game is dedicated to reaching Castle Age as quickly as possible, then exploding the economy with extra Town Centers.
- **Weak against:** Feudal all-in rushes that breach walls before Castle Age; Scout Rush that forces premature military spending.
- **Strong against:** Slow boom strategies; opponents also going for Castle Age without military pressure.

**Options:**

1. **Full Boom to Imperial:** 3+ TCs producing continuously into Imperial Age; strongest on closed maps (Arena, Black Forest).
2. **Crossbow + Siege Transition:** Once 40+ villagers are established, invest in 2–3 Archery Ranges and a Siege Workshop for immediate combat power.
3. **Knight Flood:** Add 2 Stables to the established economy for Knight production; very strong if the opponent has not built Pikemen.

**Defense Tip:**
Strongest on closed maps (Arena, Black Forest). On Arabia, requires careful walling and possibly a small defensive force in Feudal Age. Chinese, Teutons, and Vikings scale best with this strategy due to their economic bonuses. Once Castle Age hits, place the additional TCs before doing anything else.
