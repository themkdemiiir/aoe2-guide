# Fast Castle into Boom

**Goal:** Reach Castle Age as quickly as possible (~13:00), then immediately deploy 2–3 additional Town Centers to multiply villager production.
**Recommended civs:** Chinese, Britons, Franks, Teutons, Vikings

## Dark Age (0–26 Villagers)

- 6 villagers on sheep
- 4 villagers on wood
- 1 villager lures first boar
- 4 villagers on berries (House near berries + Mill)
- 1 villager lures second boar
- +4 villagers seed farms around TC
- +3 villagers to 2nd lumber camp
- +3 villagers to gold

Click Feudal Age at 26 population (~10:30).

## Feudal Preparation

- 4 villagers build Blacksmith + Market (pulled from wood or straggler trees)
- Sell excess stone at the Market if short on food or gold
- No military buildings in Feudal Age

**Research:**
- (Delay) Double-Bit Axe and Horse Collar can wait until Castle Age if pressed for time

## Feudal Age (26 Villagers)

- Click Castle Age immediately after Blacksmith and Market are placed (~13:00)
- No military production in Feudal Age
- Every resource flows toward the age-up chain

## Castle Age (28 Villagers)

- Immediately place 2–3 Town Centers near resources (wood, gold, stone)
- Research Wheelbarrow and Horse Collar to accelerate farming
- Research Bow Saw for wood efficiency
- By 20 minutes you should have 40+ villagers producing continuously

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- Fast Castle Boom is the canonical defensive/economic strategy. The entire early game is dedicated to reaching Castle Age as quickly as possible, then exploding the economy.
- **Weak against:** Feudal all-in rushes that breach walls before Castle Age; Scout Rush that forces premature military spending.
- **Strong against:** Slow boom strategies; opponents also going for Castle Age without military pressure.

**Options:**

1. **Full Boom to Imperial:** 3+ TCs producing continuously into Imperial Age; strongest on closed maps (Arena, Black Forest).
2. **Crossbow + Siege Transition:** Once 40+ villagers are established, invest in 2–3 Archery Ranges and a Siege Workshop for immediate combat power.
3. **Knight Flood:** Add 2 Stables to established economy for Knight production; very strong if opponent has not built Pikemen.

**Defense Tip:**
Strongest on closed maps (Arena, Black Forest). On Arabia, requires careful walling and possibly a small defensive force in Feudal Age. Chinese, Teutons, and Vikings scale best with this strategy due to their economic bonuses. Once Castle Age hits, place additional TCs before doing anything else.
