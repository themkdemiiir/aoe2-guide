# Fast Castle into Boom

Fast Castle into Boom is the canonical defensive/economic strategy. The entire early game is dedicated to reaching Castle Age as quickly as possible (~13:00), then immediately deploying 2-3 additional Town Centers to multiply villager production.

No Feudal Age military — no stable, no range. Every resource goes to food, wood, and just enough gold for the age-up chain. Blacksmith and Market are built in Feudal Age to satisfy Castle requirements; sell excess stone at the Market if short on food or gold.

Once Castle hits, immediately place 2–3 additional TCs near resources. Research Wheelbarrow and Horse Collar to accelerate farming. By 20 minutes you should have 40+ villagers.

Strongest on closed maps (Arena, Black Forest). On Arabia, requires careful walling and possibly a small defensive force. Chinese, Teutons, and Vikings scale best.
