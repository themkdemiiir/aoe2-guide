# 18 Pop Scouts into Cavalry Archers

Open with Scouts for map control, wall up safely behind them, then hide a Cavalry Archer mass behind your walls in Castle Age.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- The Scouts in Feudal are a screen, not the win condition — they buy time and map control while you wall completely and tech toward the real payoff.
- Walling is non-negotiable: a sealed base lets you transition greedily into Cavalry Archers without dying to a counterattack.
- In Castle Age redistribute your economy, add gold and a second Range, and research Gold Mining, Bow Saw, Bodkin Arrow, and Husbandry so the Cavalry Archers hit hard and ride fast.
- Strong against static, defensive opponents and slow armies that cannot chase mobile ranged units; weak against a heavy Skirmisher response, so keep some Spearmen and never overcommit Scouts.
- Late game, fold into Cavalry Archers plus Hussars or Cavalry Archers plus Halberdiers, backed by Siege, for a flexible mid- and post-imperial composition.
