# 17 Pop Teuton Tower Rush

Reach Feudal at 17 pop, march ten villagers forward, and choke the enemy economy under a chain of cheap, garrison-stuffed Teuton towers.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- This is a committal opening, so scouting comes before the click-up: locate the enemy first so your ten forward villagers land on their resources instead of marching into walls or open map.
- Teutons are the definitive tower civilization — their towers are cheaper, garrison up to ten villagers, and fire back hard, so a Teuton tower reliably wins any tower-versus-tower exchange and is painful to dislodge.
- Hold every eco upgrade (Double-Bit Axe, Horse Collar) until the first tower is up; standing stone and bodies on the front line matter far more than economy polish this early.
- Once the first tower stands, keep walking: chain towers 6–8 tiles apart to peel the enemy off each food and wood patch in turn and slowly suffocate their gathering.
- If they wall up before you arrive, roll the pressure into Castle Age — a forward Stable for Knights or a Siege Workshop with Mangonels behind your tower line keeps them pinned.
- Pull wounded villagers inside a tower to save them, and never let home production idle; the rush only pays off if your Town Center keeps pumping while the towers go up.
