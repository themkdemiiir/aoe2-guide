# Armenian Spear Rush into Fast Castle Relic Control

Poke with early Spearmen to deny the enemy opening, then Fast Castle into a Fortified Church that floods the map with Warrior Priests and locks down the relics.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- The two or three early Spearmen plus your Scout are a low-cost insurance package: they shut down an opponent's Scout opening and buy the time your Fast Castle needs, without bleeding real resources off the climb.
- Wall as you go. A slow palisade with three villagers around the boar and hunt keeps the build safe so the Spear pressure never has to convert into a full defensive stand.
- Out of Castle Age, drop a Fortified Church in a defensible spot and immediately spam Warrior Priests — their job is to grab relics, not to fight. Aim to lock down as many as six relics for a long gold trickle that funds everything that follows.
- Take Bow Saw early so the wood economy keeps up with non-stop Warrior Priest and building production, on top of the Double-Bit Axe and Horse Collar you queued on the way up.
- This is an Armenian-only plan: their Fortified Church and Warrior Priests are what make the relic lockdown possible, so the build does not transfer to other civilizations.
- Relic income is a slow burn — protect the Church and the priests with walls and a few Spearmen, and convert the steady gold into whatever Castle Age army your matchup demands.
