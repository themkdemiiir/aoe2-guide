# Man-at-Arms Into Skirms

Hit Feudal Age around 9:15 with Militia already walking out, upgrade to Man-at-Arms, and back them with Skirmishers for a full-Feudal composition that beats most early army mixes.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- This is a pure Feudal Age power play: Man-at-Arms do the damage while Skirmishers shield them from enemy Archers, giving you an army that answers almost anything an opponent can field before Castle Age.
- Get the first three Militia out the moment you age up and start poking at the enemy's Villagers and woodlines — early pressure forces them onto defense and buys time for your eco to rebound from the aggressive opener.
- Lead with Man-at-Arms and Double-Bit Axe, then layer in Skirmishers as soon as you see Archers; the two units cover each other's weaknesses, so keep the front line of melee in front of your ranged support.
- Tech up steadily at the Blacksmith — Fletching first, then Infantry and Skirmisher armor — and add Arson once you have seven or eight Man-at-Arms so they tear through buildings and snowball any push.
- Do not forget the economy behind the fight: seed farms off the dwindling hunt, take Wheelbarrow at 14-16 farms, and keep walling so the constant unit production never stalls your food or wood.
- From a winning Feudal position you can keep grinding in Feudal or transition smoothly to Castle Age, folding the army and farm economy into Crossbows, Pikemen, or a Knight switch depending on the matchup.
