# Khmer Fast Castle Knights and Scorpions

**Goal:** Exploit Khmer's no-building-prerequisite bonus to skip Feudal structures entirely and slam into Castle Age (~13:30) with an aggressive Knight-and-Scorpion push.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- Khmer's signature trick: villagers never need to walk to a building to advance, so you click Castle the instant Feudal finishes without spending wood on a Barracks or Stable. That saved time and resources is the whole point of this build.
- Defend the dangerous Feudal-to-Castle window with hidden houses and your Scout — you have no military yet, so position your Town Center and walls to deny easy villager kills.
- The moment you reach Castle, build a Stable and Blacksmith together, take +1 cavalry armor, and start non-stop Knights; a forward Siege Workshop lets Scorpions clear massed archers and add map pressure.
- Scorpions are the Khmer power spike here — their bonus damage shreds infantry and archer clumps while Knights punish anything that tries to close in.
- **Weak against:** Camel and Pikeman compositions that hard-counter cavalry; an opponent who walls perfectly and matches your Castle timing with Crossbows plus Pikes.
- **Follow-up:** Add Town Centers behind the push if it stalls, then transition toward Elite Battle Elephants or a relic-backed Monk play in the late game.
