# Men-at-Arms into Archers

**Goal:** Disrupt the enemy economy with 3 Men-at-Arms in Feudal Age while a wave of Archers arrives behind them.
**Recommended civs:** Bulgarians (free Blacksmith), Malians (infantry attack bonus), Celts (faster infantry), Berbers, Goths (cheaper infantry)

## Dark Age (0–21 Villagers)

- First 6 villagers to sheep under the TC; build 2 houses
- 3 villagers to wood; build Lumber Camp
- 1 villager lures the Boar to the TC
- 2 villagers build a Mill at the berries
- 3 villagers to berries
- 2 villagers to sheep/boar; research Loom
- 1 villager builds the Barracks, then a Farm (vill 19)
- 2 villagers build a Mining Camp on gold and start mining (vill 20–21)

Click Feudal Age at 21 pop (~10:30). As you advance, pull 4 villagers off sheep to build a 2nd Lumber Camp and queue 3 Militia at the Barracks.

## Feudal Preparation

- Research Men-at-Arms at the Barracks the moment Feudal lands
- Build 2 Archery Ranges and a Blacksmith
- Send the 3 Men-at-Arms into the opponent's wood line

**Research:** Double-Bit Axe (Lumber Camp), Fletching (Blacksmith, after the first few Archers)

## Feudal Age (21+ Villagers)

- Train 6 Archers non-stop from both Ranges
- Men-at-Arms snipe isolated villagers at the wood line and stragglers
- New villagers fill Farms, wood, and gold as the economy shifts
- Transition Men-at-Arms back to defend once Archers are numerous

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

The MAA + Archers combo is the strongest all-in feudal attack. Men-at-Arms force the opponent to spend resources on Spearmen rather than economy, and the Archers behind them clean up whatever the infantry pins down.

- **Weak against:** Heavy Skirmisher spam (counters Archers), fast walling plus a Spearman response.
- **Strong against:** Pure Scout opens, passive Feudal economies, anyone who doesn't react to MAA in the first 2 minutes.

**Options:**

1. **Crossbowman + Siege:** Research Crossbowman and add Mangonels — the standard Castle Age continuation.
2. **Knight switch:** Drop Archers and go 2-Stable Knights if the opponent commits to pure Skirmishers.
3. **All-in Feudal:** Add a 2nd batch of MAA if the opponent is crippled.
