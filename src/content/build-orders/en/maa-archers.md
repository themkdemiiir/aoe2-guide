# Men-at-Arms into Archers

**Goal:** Disrupt the enemy economy with 3 Men-at-Arms in Feudal Age while Archers arrive behind them.
**Recommended civs:** Bulgarians (free Blacksmith), Malians (infantry attack bonus), Celts (faster infantry), Berbers, Goths (cheaper infantry)

## Dark Age (0–18 Villagers)

- 6 villagers on sheep
- 2 villagers on wood; build Lumber Camp
- 1 villager builds Barracks (near your base, slightly forward)
- 3 villagers to berries; build Mill
- 2 more villagers to wood (14 total)
- 4 more villagers to wood (18 total)
- Research Loom

Queue 3 Militia the moment you have food. Click Feudal Age at 18 pop (~9:30).

## Feudal Preparation

- Upgrade Militia to Men-at-Arms as soon as Feudal arrives
- Build 2 Archery Ranges
- Send Men-at-Arms into opponent's wood line

**Research:** Double-Bit Axe, Fletching (after first 3–4 Archers)

## Feudal Age (18 Villagers)

- Produce Archers non-stop from both Ranges
- 2 new TC villagers to gold; Mining Camp
- Men-at-Arms target isolated villagers at the wood line
- Transition Men-at-Arms back once Archers are numerous

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

The MAA + Archers combo is the strongest all-in feudal attack. Men-at-Arms force the opponent to spend resources on Spearmen rather than economy.

- **Weak against:** Heavy Skirmisher spam (counters Archers), fast walled + Spearman response.
- **Strong against:** Pure Scout opens, passive Feudal economies, anyone who doesn't react to MAA in the first 2 minutes.

**Options:**

1. **Crossbow + Siege:** Research Crossbow + add Mangonels — standard Castle Age continuation.
2. **Knight switch:** Drop Archers, go 2-Stable Knights if opponent goes pure Skirmishers.
3. **All-in Feudal:** Add a 2nd batch of MAA if opponent is crippled.
