# Scout Rush (20 Pop Feudal)

**Goal:** Reach Feudal Age at 20 population with a Stable ready, then flood the map with Scout Cavalry to harass enemy villagers before they wall.
**Recommended civs:** Franks, Huns, Lithuanians, Magyars, Malians

## Dark Age (0–20 Villagers)

- 6 villagers on sheep (build 2 houses with your early villagers)
- 3 villagers on wood; villager 7 builds the lumber camp
- 1 villager lures the first boar, 1 more on sheep/boar
- 2 villagers build 2 houses near the berries, then a Mill
- 2 villagers on berries
- 2 villagers on sheep/boar (lure the 2nd boar when needed)
- 3 villagers build a 2nd lumber camp, then chop wood
- Research Loom

Click Feudal Age at 20 population.

## Feudal Preparation

- Take 4 villagers off sheep to build a Barracks, then a Stable
- Train 2–3 Scout Cavalry first and send them toward the opponent's base
- Build Farms as the sheep/boar food runs out
- Send Stable builders to palisade walls after construction

**Research:**

- Double-Bit Axe (Lumber Camp)
- Horse Collar (Mill) — keeps farmers gathering as you transition off berries

## Feudal Age (20 Villagers)

- Produce Scout Cavalry continuously from the Stable
- New villagers from the TC go to gold; build a Mining Camp
- Research Bloodlines after 7–8 Scouts are out
- Keep adding Farms as food allows
- Produce 1–2 Spearmen if the opponent also has Scouts

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- The Scout Rush is the foundational aggressive opening in AoE2. Keep production constant: a Scout should leave the Stable the moment food allows, and your villagers never idle.
- **Weak against:** Archer openings (Archers beat Scouts in Feudal); opponents who wall fast and produce Spearmen.
- **Strong against:** Opponents who don't wall; greedy Feudal Age economies without early military.

**Options:**

1. **2-Stable Knights:** The standard Castle Age follow-up — add a 2nd Stable and produce Knights for sustained pressure.
2. **Pivot to Skirmishers:** If the opponent has Crossbowmen, produce Elite Skirmishers to counter before transitioning to Castle Age.
3. **Feudal All-In:** If opponent has no walls and no Spearmen, flood 8+ Scouts for immediate kill pressure before they can respond.

**Defense Tip:**
Common beginner mistakes are forgetting to lure the second boar, building the Stable too late, and neglecting palisade walls. Note: Franks do NOT have Bloodlines — research Husbandry instead for speed. Huns require no houses, freeing wood for earlier Stable construction.
