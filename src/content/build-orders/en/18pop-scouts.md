# 1-Stable Scouts

Land in Feudal at 18 pop around 8:50 with a single stable, then pump Scout Cavalry to harass enemy villagers while your economy snowballs.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- Production is everything: a scout should leave the stable the moment you have the food, and your TC should never sit idle. The 18-pop landing buys you tempo, so spend it pressuring the opponent rather than sitting at home.
- React to what your scout sees. Versus archers or spears, add an archery range and skirmishers; versus enemy scouts, add your own scouts plus spears until you are fully walled.
- Tech up your cavalry once it is rolling. Build a blacksmith, and research Bloodlines after 7–8 scouts are on the field so you are not delaying military for it.
- Default Castle Age plan is 2-stable Knights for sustained pressure. Against a crossbow mass, peel a single range into elite skirmishers; on open maps where you just need map control, light cavalry keeps the harass alive.
- Civ notes: Franks lack Bloodlines, so lean on Husbandry for speed; Huns need no houses, freeing wood to start the stable a touch earlier.
