# Khmer Super Fast Scouts

Abuse the Khmer free-housing bonus to reach Feudal at 16 pop around 7:35 and flood the map with Scout Cavalry before your opponent can react.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- The whole point is speed: a 16-pop, all-food landing gets scouts on the map well ahead of a standard 18-pop opening, so the first minutes of Feudal should be spent hunting villagers, not idling at home.
- Khmer skip Feudal infrastructure they do not need. There is no barracks and no wall reflex here — vils tuck into nearby houses thanks to the garrison bonus, so place houses on the resources you most want to defend.
- Keep the stable and TC producing in lockstep. Wood goes to the lumber camp first, then farms once you bank 60, while the +6 berry villagers keep enough food coming to never pause Scout Cavalry.
- React to your scouting. Add spearmen only if the opponent commits to their own scouts; against archers, peel toward an archery range and skirmishers instead of forcing more cavalry into a bad trade.
- Carry the tempo into Castle Age. Because Khmer pay no wood for houses, the saved resources can snowball into Knights or a fast follow-up tech spike if your early harassment lands.
