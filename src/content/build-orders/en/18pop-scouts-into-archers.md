# 18 Pop Scouts into Archers

Open with Scout Cavalry pressure off an 18-pop Feudal landing, then surprise the opponent with an Archer switch once the harass has them committed to anti-cavalry play.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- The plan baits an over-reaction: early Scouts push the enemy toward Spearmen and walls, and the moment they over-invest in anti-cavalry you flip to Archers from two Ranges, which their Spears cannot answer.
- Keep at least one Spearman of your own in production to screen against mirror Scout play while your economy reslots onto wood and gold for the Archer phase.
- Time the gold move carefully: pull the 8 Town Center villagers onto gold and a Mining Camp only once farms and berries cover food, so Archer production never stalls.
- Blacksmith into Fletching is the key Archer power-spike here; prioritize it over extra eco upgrades once arrows start flying.
- Chinese, Huns, Malians, and Portuguese all suit this build: Chinese for the extra starting villagers, Huns for skipping houses, Malians for cheaper farm upgrades, and Portuguese for cheaper gold-cost units.
- From here you can all-in Feudal with a second Stable plus Bloodlines, or stabilize the economy and carry the Archer mass into a strong Castle Age Crossbow timing.
