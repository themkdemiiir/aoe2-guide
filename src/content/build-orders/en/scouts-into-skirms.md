# 18 Pop Scouts into Skirms

Open with Scout Cavalry off an 18-pop Feudal landing, then layer in Skirmishers the moment the opponent shows Archers, giving you a cheap answer to both ranged play and Spearman responses.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- Scouts come first to pressure villagers and force a reaction; the Skirmisher switch is reactive, so only commit to the Range once you actually see the enemy leaning on Archers.
- Skirmishers are gold-free and hard-counter Archers, so this opening shrugs off the most common Feudal aggression while your Scouts keep punishing anything that steps outside walls.
- Keep the economy on wood and food early — Skirmishers and Scouts cost no gold, which lets you delay the gold move until berries are spent and you split 6 onto a Mining Camp.
- Blacksmith into Fletching plus infantry armor is the key power-spike: it lifts your Skirmishers above the enemy's Archers and keeps your Scouts alive in the skirmish.
- Franks, Huns, Malians, and Lithuanians all fit: Franks for hardier cavalry and free farm upgrades, Huns for skipping houses, Malians for cheaper farming, and Lithuanians for the early food bonus that speeds the whole opening.
- In Castle Age this transitions cleanly into Elite Skirmishers backed by Knights — the Skirmishers screen against Crossbows while the Knights provide the punch your gold-light Feudal lacked.
