# Chinese Fast Feudal

Exploit the Chinese extra starting villagers and house-free opening to reach Feudal Age fast and pick whichever aggression your scouting demands.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- The Chinese start with three extra villagers and no houses to build, so this opener stacks bodies onto food earlier than any other civ and clicks up around 8:25 already two villagers ahead of a standard 20-pop landing.
- Because the eco lands so far in front, the build is deliberately open-ended: scout your opponent first, then commit to the line their setup punishes least.
- Scouts are the safe default into uncertainty — pair the Stable with eco upgrades so the lead snowballs while the Cavalry pokes for damage.
- Archers want a little gold setup first; drop a Mining Camp and pull 2-3 villagers before the Range so production never starves, then layer Fletching on top.
- Man-at-Arms pressure leans on three quick Militia plus the upgrade and Double-Bit Axe; skip the full MAA-into-tower-rush, since the early eco cannot fund both at once.
- A pure Tower Rush sends five villagers forward into the enemy gold or wood — devastating when they are unwalled, but only gamble on it once your scout confirms an opening.
