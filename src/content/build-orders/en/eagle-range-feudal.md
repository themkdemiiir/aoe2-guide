# Eagle and Range Feudal Rush

Hit Feudal fast off a heavy hunt, then apply two-pronged pressure with Eagle Scouts plus an Archery Range backing them up.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- This is a Mesoamerican-only opener (Aztecs, Incas, Mayans): they are the civs with Eagle Scouts, and their food bonuses let the boar-and-deer-heavy collection land Feudal near 9:15.
- The whole idea is two threats at once — Eagles run down stragglers and raid villagers while your Range adds ranged damage the enemy must also answer, splitting their defense.
- Read your opponent before committing the second unit: against an archer or scout opening, mass your own Archers; against a skirmisher wall, switch the same villagers to Skirmishers so you out-trade them.
- Wall early with the Range and Barracks builders — Houses and palisades close the gaps so your raiders stay safe and the enemy cannot counter-raid your exposed gold.
- Take Double-Bit Axe but skip Horse Collar here: wood feeds the Range and walls, and your hunt covers food long enough to delay Farm tech.
- To transition, seed Farms behind the army, keep gold flowing for Eagles, and add Fletching plus Blacksmith upgrades before deciding whether to keep pressuring or boom into Castle Age.
