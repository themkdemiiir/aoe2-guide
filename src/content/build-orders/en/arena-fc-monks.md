# Arena Fast Castle with Monk Rush

**Goal:** Fast Castle with no Feudal military, send the scout out the gate immediately to grab relics, then mass Monks and Siege in Castle Age.
**Recommended civs:** Aztecs (+5 relic gold/sec), Byzantines (cheap Greek Fire + Cataphracts), Bohemians (faster Monastery tech), Spanish (free conversion gold), Saracens (Monks heal faster)

## Dark Age (0–23 Villagers)

- 6 villagers on sheep
- 3 villagers on wood; Lumber Camp
- 3 villagers to berries; Mill
- 3 more villagers to wood
- 8 more villagers to food/wood

**Critical:** Send the scout out your stone wall gate the moment it spawns. First player to grab 3+ relics wins the gold war. Aim for scout at gate before reaching 10 villagers.

Click Feudal Age at 23 population (~10:30). Research NO Feudal military.

## Feudal Preparation

- Build Blacksmith + Market
- 4 villagers to gold; Mining Camp
- Boom to 28–30 villagers
- Research Double-Bit Axe, Horse Collar

## Castle Age (28 Villagers)

- Build Monastery immediately; produce 2 Monks; grab remaining relics
- Build a Castle (defense + unique unit access)
- Build Siege Workshop; produce Battering Rams for wall breaching
- Research Sanctity, Fervor, Redemption
- Add 2nd Town Center; boom to 40+ villagers

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

The Monk + Siege combination is the strongest Arena strategy. 5 relics generate 100 gold/minute permanently — a near-insurmountable advantage.

- **Weak against:** Massed archers killing Monks; Trebuchet snipes on unsupported Monks; opponent grabbing 4+ relics first.
- **Strong against:** Knight-heavy armies (Monks convert Knights); slow infantry; opponents who ignore monastery protection.

**Options:**

1. **Unique Unit spam:** Build a 2nd Castle and mass your unique unit for a power spike.
2. **Fast Imperial:** Relic gold finances Imperial Age at ~28–30 minutes.
3. **Castle Drop:** Forward a Castle near opponent's wall perimeter for a 2-front fight.
