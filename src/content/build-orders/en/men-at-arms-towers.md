# Men-at-Arms + Tower Rush

**Goal:** Control your opponent's access to resources by placing towers in strategic locations in their base and severely restrict their economy.
**Recommended civs:** The source guide is civ-agnostic — any civ can run it. It works best with civilizations that have cheap or strong towers (Koreans, Incas, Japanese) or extra resources to fund the forward commitment (Ethiopians, Vikings).

## Dark Age (0–21 Villagers)

- Queue villagers continuously and build 2 Houses at the start
- Send villagers to sheep until you have 6
- 1 villager builds a Lumber Camp; +3 villagers collect wood
- 1 villager lures the nearest boar
- 1 villager builds 2 Houses near the berries; 1 villager builds a Mill next to them
- +2 villagers to berries; lure the 2nd boar
- +3 villagers to sheep or boar food
- 1 villager builds a Barracks, trains 3 Militia, then goes to mine Stone
- 1 villager builds a Mining Camp next to your gold; 1 villager mines gold

Research Loom in your Town Center, then advance to the Feudal Age once your gold is banked and the 3 Militia are trained.

## Feudal Preparation

- Scout your opponent's base well to find their main gold and woodlines before going forward
- Upgrade Militia to Men-at-Arms as soon as Feudal arrives
- Send the Militia/Men-at-Arms plus your Stone and Gold villagers forward toward the enemy base

**Research:** Loom (Dark Age)

## Feudal Age (21 Villagers)

- Build your first tower out of your opponent's reach — it becomes the defense for your second tower
- Place towers on the enemy's gold and wood lines to deny those resources
- Use Men-at-Arms to chase isolated villagers and to screen your tower builders
- Pull low-HP villagers into a finished tower to garrison and shoot
- Build Houses forward if you run short on population space

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

A successful tower rush is one of the biggest early-game disruptions you can create — it forces the opponent out of important resources or makes them rebuild buildings entirely. The Men-at-Arms protect your forward villagers and harass while the towers convert that tempo into lasting map control.

- **Weak against:** Quick reactions — this attack requires a large commitment (around 5 villagers not gathering resources) and leaves you with a big economic setback if it fails. Opponents who scout early and wall before your villagers arrive blunt the whole plan.
- **Strong against:** Greedy and passive openings that cannot respond to forward aggression in time, and economies clustered on exposed gold or wood lines.

**Notes from the build:**

- Scout your opponent's base well to find their main gold and woodlines before going forward.
- The first tower you build will be your second tower's defense, so try to build it out of your opponent's reach.

**Cautions:**

- This is a fairly complex build that needs practice to execute properly; it gets especially tricky micro-managing forward villagers and military while expanding your economy at home.
- The large commitment means a failed rush is a significant economic setback — only commit once you have scouted a good target.

**Options:**

1. **Tower creep into Castle Age:** Keep chaining towers and transition the home economy into a Castle Age timing once the enemy is contained.
2. **Archery Range follow-up:** Add an Archery Range behind your towers and produce Archers to support the Men-at-Arms.
3. **Recover economy:** Once the forward resources are locked down, add Double-Bit Axe and Horse Collar to rebuild your eco and snowball the lead.
