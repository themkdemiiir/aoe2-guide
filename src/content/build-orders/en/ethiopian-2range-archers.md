# Ethiopian 2-Range Archers for Team Games

Land in Feudal at 18 pop and open two Archery Ranges at once, leaning on Ethiopia's faster Archers and free age-up resources to flood the flank with arrows.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- This is a flank build for team games: two ranges from the start means double the Archer output, and Ethiopia's Archers move 15% faster, so they kite Skirmishers and disengage from Scouts better than a generic ranged opening.
- The Dark Age is hunt-heavy — one boar, a deer lure, then a second boar — so you reach Feudal with almost no wood banked. Get the new lumber camp and straggler vils onto wood immediately or Archer production will stall.
- Push gold hard. Seven villagers go to gold in Feudal so Fletching lands fast and you never run dry feeding two ranges; Ethiopia's free +100 food and +100 gold on each age-up cushions the heavy military spend and speeds the click to Castle.
- This opening deliberately sacrifices economy for tempo. Add berries and farms only as your hunt expires, sell spare stone at the Market to push the Castle click, and keep raw Archer numbers climbing rather than over-investing in eco upgrades.
- In Castle Age go straight for Crossbow and Bodkin Arrow with Bow Saw to keep the wood flowing; a second lumber camp on six vils sustains the mass. Let your pocket ally carry the eco boom while you apply constant pressure on the flank.
