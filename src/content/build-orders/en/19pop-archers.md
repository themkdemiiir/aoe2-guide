# 19 Pop 1-Range Archers

The 19 pop Archer Rush is the foundational ranged opening alongside scouts. Reaching Feudal at 9:15, it prioritizes a fast archery range and the Fletching upgrade over military quantity.

Key milestones: lumber camp very early (3 wood vils by pop 9), second boar lured promptly, Feudal click at 19 pop with 5 wood banked, archery range up within 30 seconds of Feudal landing, Fletching researched ASAP. Gold mining camp goes up during Feudal — 2 villagers on gold is enough initially.

The biggest beginner error is delaying the archery range or gold mining. If you spot enemy scouts, 1-4 spearmen from the barracks doesn't disrupt the build significantly.

In Castle Age, transition to Crossbowmen with Bodkin Arrow. Britons should add second and third archery ranges in early Castle Age and push for Thumb Ring.
