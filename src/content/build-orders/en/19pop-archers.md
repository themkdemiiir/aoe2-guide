# 19 Pop 1-Range Archers

**Goal:** Reach Feudal Age at 19 population, build one Archery Range immediately, and begin producing Archers with Fletching as quickly as possible.
**Recommended civs:** Britons, Mayans, Ethiopians, Tatars, Vikings

## Dark Age (0–19 Villagers)

- 6 villagers on sheep
- 3 villagers on wood (lumber camp very early — 3 wood vils by pop 9)
- 1 villager lures nearest boar
- 1 villager builds a House then works berries
- +6 villagers hunting (lure 2nd boar promptly)
- +2 villagers to straggler trees
- Research Loom

Click Feudal Age at 19 population (~9:15).

## Feudal Preparation

- Send 5 hunting villagers to 2nd lumber camp (7 total lumberjacks)
- 2 villagers to straggler trees
- 1 villager builds Barracks + House
- 2 villagers go to gold, build Mining Camp

**Research:**

- Double-Bit Axe
- (Optional) Horse Collar

## Feudal Age (19 Villagers)

- Build Archery Range within 30 seconds of Feudal arriving
- Begin producing Archers immediately
- Build Blacksmith and research Fletching as soon as possible
- +2 more villagers to gold (4 total)
- Produce 1–4 Spearmen from Barracks if opponent sends Scouts
- Continue new villagers to gold/wood/farms as needed

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- The Archer Rush is the foundational ranged opening alongside Scouts. Fletching is mandatory before committing Archers to battle.
- **Weak against:** Scout Cavalry (Scouts beat Archers in open field before Fletching); Skirmisher spam (Skirmishers hard-counter Archers).
- **Strong against:** Greedy economies; opponents without early military response; slow-walling opponents.

**Options:**

1. **Crossbow Castle Transition:** The standard follow-up — advance to Castle Age with Bodkin Arrow for a strong Crossbow timing attack. As food sources deplete, research Horse Collar and shift villagers onto Farms while keeping a steady gold income to sustain ranged production.
2. **Feudal All-In:** Add a 2nd Archery Range and flood Archers; works if opponent has no Skirmisher counter.
3. **Britons Double Range:** Britons should add 2nd and 3rd Archery Ranges in early Castle Age and push for Thumb Ring for maximum Archer production.

**Defense Tip:**
The biggest beginner error is delaying the Archery Range or gold mining. If you spot enemy Scouts, producing 1–4 Spearmen from the Barracks does not significantly disrupt the build. In Castle Age, always research Bodkin Arrow before sending Crossbowmen into battle.
