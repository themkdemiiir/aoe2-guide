# 19 Pop 1-Range Archers

Land in Feudal at 19 pop, throw down a single Archery Range, and pump Fletching-backed Archers before your opponent has a counter ready.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- This is the bread-and-butter ranged opening: one range, Double-Bit Axe to feed wood into Archers, and an early Blacksmith so Fletching is done before your first volley connects. Skip Horse Collar in Feudal — you want the wood and food going into military, not farm upgrades yet.
- Keep gold healthy. Two miners go down on the click-up, two more follow shortly after; four on gold is enough to sustain steady Archer production without starving your wood line.
- React to what you see. If the enemy commits to Scouts, a handful of Spearmen from the barracks plugs the hole cheaply; if they go Skirmishers, do not over-invest in Archers — bank resources and lean on the Castle transition instead.
- Default follow-up is Castle Age with 10–15 Archers, then Crossbow plus Bodkin Arrow for a hard timing attack. Add a second Archery Range or a Stable depending on the matchup.
- Britons can lean hardest into this: extra range from the civ bonus means a third range and Thumb Ring in Castle Age turn the Archer mass into a snowball.
