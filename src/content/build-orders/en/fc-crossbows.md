# Fast Castle into Crossbows

**Goal:** Reach Castle Age with ~26 villagers, then mass Crossbowmen and Siege to dominate mid-game.
**Recommended civs:** Britons (+1 range), Ethiopians (+2 pierce attack), Vietnamese (see opponent on start), Mayans (+25% archer HP), Chinese (extra starting resources)

## Dark Age (0–18 Villagers)

- 6 villagers on sheep
- 3 villagers on wood; Lumber Camp
- 3 villagers to berries; Mill
- 2 more villagers to wood (14 total)
- 4 more villagers to wood/sheep (18 total)
- No military — pure economy focus

Click Feudal Age at 18 population (~9:00).

## Feudal Preparation

- Build Blacksmith + Market (Castle Age prerequisites)
- 2 villagers to gold; Mining Camp
- Continue TC production to 26 villagers
- Add Farms; research Double-Bit Axe

Click Castle Age at ~26 population (~13:30).

## Castle Age (26 Villagers)

- Build 2 Archery Ranges immediately
- Research Crossbow (high priority)
- Produce Crossbowmen non-stop from both Ranges
- Build Siege Workshop for Mangonel support
- Add more gold villagers as queue grows

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

FC Crossbows is dominant in team games where the pocket has time to boom. Crossbow + Mangonel is very hard to counter without cavalry.

- **Weak against:** Knight rushes arriving before Mangonels are out; Skirmisher + cavalry mixes.
- **Strong against:** Infantry armies, other archer civs (out-ranges them with upgrades).

**Options:**

1. **Trebs + Crossbow:** Add Trebuchets for siege while Crossbows protect them.
2. **Switch to Arbalest:** Upgrade in Imperial for maximum late-game output.
3. **Add Cavalry:** If opponent masses Skirmishers, add Knights to kill them.
