# Fire Galleys and Archers

**Goal:** Control the water with Fire Galleys while applying land pressure with Archers, using a Fishing Ship economy to fund a smooth two-front push into Castle Age.
**Recommended civs:** Italians (cheaper Docks + ships, cheaper Fire Galleys), Japanese (faster-training warships, strong archers), Vikings (cheaper, tankier navy), Byzantines (cheap counter-units, strong Fire Ship line), Portuguese (+HP ships, gold discount)

## Dark Age (0–21 Villagers)

- 3 villagers build 2 houses, then to sheep; queue villagers non-stop at the Town Center
- 4 villagers to wood with a Lumber Camp
- Lure the first boar around 11–12; build a Dock near deep fish plus a second house
- Queue 4 Fishing Ships from the Dock onto the deep fish
- More villagers to wood; lure the second boar; spread spare villagers across sheep and boar
- Research Loom, then click Feudal Age at 21 population
- While advancing: pull a second Lumber Camp from straggler trees, build a Gold Mining Camp (4 villagers), and place a Barracks toward your front

## Feudal Age (21 Villagers)

- Research Double-Bit Axe in the Lumber Camp
- Train Fire Galleys from the Dock to take water control
- Build a house and 2 Archery Ranges near your front; train Archers non-stop
- New villagers to gold (toward 24); add a Mill on berries
- Build a Blacksmith and research Fletching for archer range
- Research Wheelbarrow; transition wood villagers onto Farms as fish/boar deplete
- Add a final villager to gold and click Castle Age at ~35 population

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

This is a two-front opening: Fire Galleys deny the enemy's water economy and Fishing Ships while Archers pressure their land eco. The Fishing Ship income is what lets you afford both a navy and a Range count at the same time, so protect your Dock and keep ships fishing.

- **Weak against:** Dedicated Galley floods that out-mass your Fire Galleys before they tech up; heavy Skirmisher civs that shut down the Archer half on land; opponents who out-boom on a closed map where your navy adds little.
- **Strong against:** Greedy Fast Castle players who skip Feudal military; pure-water builds caught without Fire Ship counters; land-focused civs forced to split attention between shore and front.

**Options:**

1. **Crossbows + Galleys (Castle Age):** Upgrade Archers to Crossbowmen and Fire Galleys to Fire Ships, continuing the same two-front pressure.
2. **Add Demolition Ships:** Slip Demo Ships into clustered enemy Fishing Ships or Docks for heavy splash value.
3. **Boom transition:** If you win the water, lean on the Fishing Ship economy to add Town Centers and out-eco a contained opponent.
