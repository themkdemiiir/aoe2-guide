# Knight Rush (Beginner)

Reach Castle Age on a clean economy and flood Knights from two Stables for a forgiving, high-impact attack.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- This is the most beginner-friendly aggressive opener: your eco runs on a tidy sheep-boar-berry-then-farms loop, so you can focus on macro instead of micro while you bank toward Castle Age.
- Two Stables let you keep Knights coming non-stop. Knights are tanky, fast, and punish unwalled lumber camps and gold miners, so raid the moment the first one pops.
- Get Double-Bit Axe and Horse Collar to keep wood and farm income healthy, then add Bow Saw — sustained eco upgrades matter more than rushing extra military buildings here.
- Bloodlines is a big Knight power spike for most of these civs; Franks skip it because their team-wide +20% Knight HP already covers the gap.
- Knights fold to Pikemen and Camel Riders, so scout for spears. When you see mass anti-cavalry, stop force-feeding Knights and mix in Skirmishers, a Siege Workshop, or a second Town Center to out-boom instead.
- Magyars, Slavs, Berbers, and Huns each sweeten the plan (cheaper or faster Knights, free Houses, cheaper eco), but the order is identical for all five.
