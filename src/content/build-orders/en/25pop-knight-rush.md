# Fast Castle into Knights

**Goal:** Reach Castle Age quickly with a stable economy, then immediately begin Knight production from two Stables.
**Recommended civs:** Franks, Magyars, Slavs, Berbers, Huns

## Dark Age (0–27 Villagers)

- 3 villagers build 2 Houses at the start, then all 6 onto sheep under the Town Center
- Villager 7 builds a Lumber Camp, then chops wood
- Villagers 8–10 go to wood
- Villager 11 lures the nearest boar
- Villager 12 builds 2 Houses near the berries
- Villager 13 builds a Mill next to the berries, then gathers berries
- Villagers 14–16 go to berries
- Villagers 17–18 build Farms near the TC/Mill
- Villager 19 builds a 2nd Lumber Camp, then chops wood
- Villagers 20–24 go to wood (build a House when you need population room)
- Research Loom
- Villagers 25–27 build a Mining Camp and mine gold

Click Feudal Age with the 27th villager. Build a Barracks while you advance.

## Feudal Preparation

- Build a Stable and a Blacksmith
- Shift villagers off depleted sheep onto Farms
- Pull 1 villager from wood to help build the Stable, 1 to build the Blacksmith
- (Optional) Build a Market to trade resources if your economy is unbalanced
- (Optional) Wall key chokepoints around your base

**Research:**
- Double-Bit Axe
- Horse Collar

## Castle Age (29 Villagers)

- Add a 2nd Stable
- Begin producing Knights from both Stables
- Research Bow Saw and Bloodlines, then Husbandry as you stabilize (Franks have no Bloodlines — their +20% Knight HP makes up for it)
- Keep producing villagers and maintain resource balance to sustain Knight production

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- This Fast Castle into Knights is the safest and most beginner-friendly Castle Age aggressive opener. Two Stables produce Knights at a steady rate that is hard to answer without dedicated counter units.
- **Weak against:** Pikemen and Camel Riders — these are the natural counters to Knights; Halberdiers in late game.
- **Strong against:** Archer-based economies; opponents who do not produce anti-cavalry units early.

**Options:**

1. **Knight Flood:** Keep both Stables producing Knights and add a 3rd Stable; sell resources at the Market to maintain gold flow.
2. **Knights + Siege:** Add a Siege Workshop and produce Mangonels or Battering Rams to break walls and force engagements.
3. **Extra Town Center:** If the opponent has strong defenses, add a Town Center and grow your economy; Knights remain as harassment while you out-develop them.

**Defense Tip:**
Do not let resources sit idle — use the Market to balance excess. Continuously produce Knights and villagers simultaneously. Expand your base and add extra Town Centers as your economy grows. Watch for massing Pikemen — if this happens, switch to a combined-arms approach rather than pure Knights.
