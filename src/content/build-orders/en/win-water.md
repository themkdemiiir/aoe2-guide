# Win Water

**Goal:** Open a strong fishing economy behind defensive walls, take Feudal Age at 21 villagers, and contest the water with Fire Galleys from two Docks while booming on fish.
**Recommended civs:** Vikings (cheaper Fishing Ships, +10% wood from Docks, free Bloodlines), Italians (cheaper Docks and ships, cheaper Dock techs), Portuguese (ships die slower, gold discount), Malay (cheaper Fishing Ships and a faster Fish Trap line), Japanese (cheaper Docks, +HP warships), Byzantines (cheaper Town Watch / Fire Ships line)

## Dark Age (3–21 Villagers)

- 3 villagers to sheep under the Town Center; queue Villagers non-stop and build 2 Houses with the first scouts off the TC
- 4–6 villagers to sheep
- 7–10 villagers to wood; drop a Lumber Camp on the nearest trees
- 11th villager lures the nearest Boar to the Town Center
- 12th villager builds a Dock by the deep fish, then a House, and queues 4 Fishing Ships
- 13–14 villagers to wood
- 15th villager lures the 2nd Boar
- 16–20 villagers to sheep / Boar meat
- 21st villager to sheep; research Loom and click Feudal Age

**Wall up:** While the TC pumps to 21, peel off villagers to seal your land approaches — take 1 villager from food to wood, keep 7 on wood at the Lumber Camp, put 4 on gold at a Mining Camp, send 1 to build a 2nd Dock and House, and add 2 to straggler trees.

## Feudal Age (21 Villagers)

- Research Double-Bit Axe at the Lumber Camp (+wood gather rate)
- Queue 2 Fire Galleys from the Dock to contest enemy fish and ships
- 22–23 villagers to gold mining
- 24th villager builds a Mill on Berries
- 25th villager to Berries

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

You take it from here. With a defensible land wall and Fishing Ships pumping food, you have the economy to dominate the water — the side that wins the Feudal fish-and-galley fight usually wins the game.

- **Weak against:** Opponents who match your Dock count and out-micro your Galleys; Fire Ships hard-counter the Galley line once Castle Age begins; an undefended land wall against an early land push.
- **Strong against:** Players who skip or delay their Dock; greedy fish booms with no warships; land civs forced to fight on water.

**Options:**

1. **Expand your navy:** Add more Docks and flood Galleys / Fire Ships to push the opponent off the water entirely.
2. **Fish boom:** Lean on the Fishing Ship food lead to power a fast Castle Age and out-eco your opponent.
3. **Develop a land army:** Translate the fish economy into a strong land push or a defensive force behind the walls.
