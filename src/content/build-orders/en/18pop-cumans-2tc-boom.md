# 18 Pop Cumans 2 TC Boom

Drop a second Town Center the moment you hit Feudal so your villager count snowballs ahead of any opponent who is still on one TC.

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

- The defining move is the Feudal second TC — a trick only Cumans can pull off. Get both Town Centers producing villagers without a single idle second, because every gap erases the eco lead you opened the build to chase.
- Resist the urge to add military or extra buildings early. This is a pure boom: keep new villagers flowing onto wood, stragglers, and farms, and stabilize two-TC production before you spend resources on anything else.
- Eco upgrades carry the build. Double-Bit Axe is mandatory; take Horse Collar too whenever the map feels safe, and skip it on open maps only if you are genuinely worried about a raid.
- Your one weakness is the window before the second TC pays off. A Feudal scout or man-at-arms rush can punish exposed villagers, so wall your wood and food lines early on open maps and keep your scout watching the opponent.
- Undisturbed, you reach Castle Age around 19–20 minutes with a villager count nobody on one TC can match. From there you can flood Knights, transition into whatever the matchup demands, or simply keep booming on the back of your eco lead.
