# Feudal Galley Rush

**Goal:** Reach Feudal Age quickly and flood Galleys from 2 Docks to destroy enemy Fishing Ships and deny water resources.
**Recommended civs:** Vikings (+10% wood from docks), Portuguese (slower ship death), Italians (cheaper Docks + Galleys), Byzantines (Greek Fire Ships in Castle), Malay (cheaper ships overall)

## Dark Age (0–17 Villagers)

- 4 villagers on sheep
- 2 villagers on wood; Lumber Camp near water
- 1 villager builds Dock on shoreline immediately
- 2 villagers fish (or berries if no shore fish); queue Fishing Ships for food economy
- 2 villagers to berries; Mill
- 3 more villagers to wood
- 3 more villagers to food/wood (17 total)

Click Feudal Age at 17 population (~9:00).

## Feudal Age (17 Villagers)

- Build 2nd Dock from a shore villager
- Research Fletching in Blacksmith (+1 Galley range)
- Produce Galleys non-stop from both Docks
- 3 new TC villagers to wood
- Contest enemy Docks with Galley group

## Strategy and Follow-Up (What's Next)

Water map games are won or lost in Feudal Age. A 3–4 Galley lead at Feudal arrival is nearly impossible to overcome.

- **Weak against:** Opponents who also double-dock and match Galley count; Fire Ships hard-counter Galleys in Castle Age.
- **Strong against:** Land civs forced onto water; opponents who don't build a Dock; Fishing Ship-dependent economies.

**Options:**

1. **Fire Ships (Castle Age):** Transition from Galleys to Fire Ships as Castle Age water battles begin.
2. **Land push:** Use Fishing Ship food economy to boom faster than your land opponent.
3. **Demolition Ships:** Sneak Demo Ships into enemy Dock clusters for massive splash damage.
