An 18-pop Feudal Age opening that masses Scout Cavalry to raid enemy villagers — the default open-map aggression. Scouts are fast and tanky against archers, so they harass eco and force the opponent onto the back foot while you transition.

**Related terms:** Feudal Age, Drush, Boom.
