---
slug: "galley-rush"
term: "Galley Rush (Grush)"
letter: "G"
---

Massing War Galleys early on water maps to seize control of the water, kill enemy Fishing Ships, and deny the enemy Dock. Winning the water fight chokes the opponent's economy and often decides the whole game on hybrid and water maps.

**Related terms:** Feudal Age, Boom.
