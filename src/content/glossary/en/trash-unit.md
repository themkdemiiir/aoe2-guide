---
slug: trash-unit
term: "Trash Unit"
letter: "T"
---

A unit that costs no gold — Skirmisher, Spearman/Pikeman, and Light Cavalry/Hussar. Because they only need food and wood, trash units stay sustainable in the late game when both players' gold runs out, and "trash wars" often decide long Imperial Age matches.

**Related terms:** Counter, Macro.
