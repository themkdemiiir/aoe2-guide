---
slug: uptime
term: "Uptime"
letter: "U"
---

The in-game time at which you reach the next Age (e.g. a 25:00 Castle Age uptime). Faster uptimes mean you field new units and upgrades earlier than your opponent, so shaving seconds off your build order is one of the highest-value skills to practice.

**Related terms:** Build order, Fast Castle, Idle Time.
