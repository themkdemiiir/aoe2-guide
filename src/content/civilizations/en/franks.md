Franks are a Western European civilization specializing in Cavalry.

## Civ Bonuses

- Castles are 25% cheaper
- Knights have +20% HP
- Farm upgrades are free (Mill is required to receive bonus)

## Team Bonus

## How to Play Franks

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Begin with a Scout opening to pressure the enemy economy, then transition straight into Knights once you reach Castle Age — the +20% HP bonus makes them brutally efficient. Snowball that lead by upgrading into Paladin as the game pushes toward Imperial.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Lean on massed Paladin as your core; their durability lets them trade favorably even into Halberdiers. Round the army out with a ranged answer (Skirmishers, Throwing Axemen, or Hand Cannoneers) plus your own Halberdiers to cover against enemy cavalry.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Stack Paladin in front, back them with Heavy Scorpions to shred clumped infantry, and add Trebuchets (plus Bombard Cannons) to break down defenses as you grind forward.
