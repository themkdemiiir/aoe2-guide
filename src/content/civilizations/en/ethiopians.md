Ethiopians are an Archer civilization from East Africa.

## How to Play Ethiopians

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Lean on the archer line from the start with a single-range Feudal opening, then transition into Castle Age Crossbows and quickly Arbalest. Their faster archers and the free age-up resources make pressure cheap to sustain. If you want extra punch, add a stable for a few Knights to ride forward and screen your shots or pick off enemy siege.

**Ideal late-game composition:** A practical Imperial army is Arbalest for damage, Halberdier as your free trash meatshield against cavalry, and Bombard Cannons supercharged by Torsion Engines to clear buildings and enemy siege.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Center the ball on Torsion Engine Bombard Cannons backed by Halberdiers to protect them, with Arbalest mixed in to add ranged volume as you grind forward.

**Recommended build:** [Ethiopian 2-Range Archers](/en/builds/ethiopian-2range-archers)
