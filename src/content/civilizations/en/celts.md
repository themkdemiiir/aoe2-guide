Celts are an Infantry civilization from Western Europe.

## How to Play Celts

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Lean on the faster-moving infantry and pressure early with a two-militia Feudal rush to deny your opponent's economy, then flow that aggression into Archery Ranges before transitioning toward Celtic Siege as you hit Castle Age.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Anchor your army on Halberdiers as the trash backbone and pair them with Siege — Siege Rams to crack defenses and Siege Onagers (boosted by the faster Celt reload) to clear massed enemies.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Roll forward with Halberdiers screening Woad Raiders, then back the push with Siege Rams and Siege Onagers for an unkillable infantry-and-siege ball.

**Recommended build:** [18 Pop Feudal Drush](/en/builds/18pop-feudal-drush)
