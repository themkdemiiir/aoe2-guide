- Military units created 15% faster
- +5 Monk hit points for each Monastery technology

## How to Play Aztecs

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Lean on a combined Eagle Warrior and Archery Range opening. Pair Eagles with Skirmishers when your opponent commits to archers, and back them with archers of your own against most other openings. Carry that pressure into Castle Age, where Eagles, Pikemen, and your bonus-boosted Monks let you trade efficiently and contest relics.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Transition to Elite Eagle Warriors backed by Skirmishers (with Atlatl upgrading their attack and range), and round the army out with Monks and a Trebuchet for siege. This gives you a mobile, gold-light core that still has answers to most threats.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Stack heavy Garland Wars infantry — Champions, Jaguar Warriors, and Pikemen — in front, supported by Monks for relics and conversions. Add Siege Workshop genitours or Trebuchets for ranged punch, and Bombard Cannons to crack buildings and siege.
