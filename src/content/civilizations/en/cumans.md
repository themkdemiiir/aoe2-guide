Cumans are a Cavalry civilization from Central Asia.

## How to Play Cumans

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Lean on the signature Feudal-Age second Town Center to run a 2 TC boom — your villager count outpaces opponents while you wall up and stay safe, then convert that economy into a powerful Castle-Age push.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Mass Elite Kipchaks as your gold-based ranged threat, screen them with cheap trash Hussars for raiding and to absorb hits, and add Bombard Cannons to break formations and crack down on siege.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Combine Kipchaks with Paladins for raw mobility and punch, and round out the army with Siege Rams or Trebuchets to demolish walls and buildings as you grind forward.
