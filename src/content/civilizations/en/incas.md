## How to Play Incas

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Lean on the cheaper food economy to mix Eagle Warriors with a Skirmisher-heavy Archery Range — Eagles pressure the enemy and pick off vulnerable units while Skirmishers shred any archer response. A defensive Skirmisher-plus-Spearman setup is a solid alternative when you simply need to hold ground before transitioning into the Castle Age.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Pair Kamayuks (or Elite Eagle Warriors) as your gold-and-melee core with mass Skirmishers as the cheap trash backbone, then add Trebuchets — or Caltrop-laced siege — to crack buildings and break stalemates.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Stack Kamayuks for their reach against cavalry and stacked formations, support them with Monks for relics and conversions, and roll Trebuchets forward to demolish fortified positions.
