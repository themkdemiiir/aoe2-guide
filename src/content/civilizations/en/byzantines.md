Byzantines are a Eastern Mediterranean civilization specializing in Defensive.

## Civ Bonuses

- Buildings (except gates) have +10% HP in Dark Age / +20% HP in Feudal Age  / +30% in Castle Age / +40% in Imperial Age
- Spearman skirmisher and camel lines cost 25% less
- Fire Ships attack 20% faster
- Imperial Age costs -33%
- Town Watch is free

## Team Bonus

Monks +50% heal speed

## How to Play Byzantines

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Pressure in Feudal with a Skirmisher-and-Spearman rush, leaning on your 25% discount to flood the field with cheap counter units. Carry that into Castle Age, where discounted Skirmishers, Spearmen, and Camels let you answer almost anything your opponent throws at you for a fraction of the usual cost.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Build around Halberdiers and Skirmishers as your cut-rate trash core, fold in Siege to break formations and buildings, and use your +50% heal-speed Monks to reclaim relics and recover units. Top it off with a flexible gold unit — Cataphracts, Hand Cannoneers, or Heavy Camels — depending on what the matchup demands.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** March forward behind a wall of Cataphracts, back them with Bombard Cannons to shell enemy positions, and keep Monks in the mix for healing and conversions; Bombard Towers anchor the line on defense thanks to your cheap, extra-tough buildings.

**Recommended build:** [Spearman + Skirmisher](/en/builds/korean-spear-skirm)
