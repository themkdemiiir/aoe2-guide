Georgians are a Caucasian civilization specializing in Infantry and Cavalry.

## Civ Bonuses

- Units +4 attack when garrison is not full (Mournful Shroud)
- Town Centers and Towers +2 range
- Infantry attack +1 per Era starting in Feudal Age
- Monasteries cost -50% stone

## Team Bonus

Monks +3 attack

## Unique Units

- **Monaspa** — Unique unit of the Georgians

## Unique Techs

- **Svan Towers** (Castle Age) — Towers +1 attack per 2 garrisoned units
- **Aznauri Cavalry** (Imperial Age) — Monaspa heal nearby cavalry

## How to Play Georgians

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Open with a Scout Rush, leaning on the civ's quick uptime. Your self-healing Scouts excel at hit-and-run raids — poke at enemy villagers, retreat to recover, and repeat without bleeding resources on repairs. Transition into massed Monaspa once you reach the Castle Age while your economy snowballs behind cheap Monasteries and extra Town Centers.
