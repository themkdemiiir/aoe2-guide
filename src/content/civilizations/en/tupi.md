Tupi are an Archer and Raiding civilization from South America.

## How to Play Tupi

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Lean on the archer identity early — push through Feudal with a one-range archer opening, since the +1 attack versus cavalry and the team bonus make your bowmen punch above their cost. Transition into Castle Age crossbows while using forests as a defensive crutch, where Ambush gives your archers extra range to win woodline fights.
