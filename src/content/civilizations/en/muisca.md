Muisca are a Gold and Infantry civilization from South America.

## How to Play Muisca

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Lean on the cheap, durable barracks line and the strong gold economy. Open with Men-at-Arms or Eagle Warriors backed by your faster gold income, since the -10% gold cost and +5 HP make early infantry trades favorable; pair them with a Range or extra Barracks pressure and transition into a Castle where Zipa's Authority turns your infantry into a real threat.
