Sicilians are an Infantry and Cavalry civilization from Southern Europe.

## How to Play Sicilians

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Two main paths work. The aggressive route is a Fast Castle into Serjeants, leaning on the Donjon for an all-in pressure build that punishes greedy opponents. The safer, more flexible route is a Scout opening backed by Spearmen, which keeps you mobile while transitioning smoothly into Castle Age.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Settle into Hauberk Cavalier as your gold core, paired with Skirmishers as cheap ranged trash and Trebuchets to break buildings. A viable alternative swaps the cavalry for Serjeants supported by Halberdiers and Trebuchets.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Mass Serjeants as the frontline, screen them with Scorpion-Corps genitours for ranged support, and add Trebuchets to dismantle fortifications as you grind forward.

**Recommended build:** [Scout Rush](/en/builds/18pop-scouts)
