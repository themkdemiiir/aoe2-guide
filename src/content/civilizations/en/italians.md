Italians are a Southern European civilization specializing in Archer and naval.

## Civ Bonuses

- Advance to next age costs -15%
- All dock tech costs -50%
- Fishing ship cost -15 wood
- Gunpowder unit costs -25%

## Team Bonus

Condottiero also available in allies' barracks in Imperial Age (once you have built a castle)

## How to Play Italians

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Italians are flexible early — open either with a Scout Rush or with single-range Archers, since both scale into the mid-game thanks to the cheaper age-up. Avoid committing to infantry and instead prioritize reaching the Castle Age quickly to unlock your stronger gold units.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Lean on Genoese Crossbowmen as your gold core, backed by Hussars as your trash line, with Bombard Cannons providing siege.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Build around massed Genoese Crossbowmen with Condottieri to absorb and counter enemy gold units, add Monks for support, and round it out with Bombard Cannons for siege power.
