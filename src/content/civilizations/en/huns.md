Huns are a Central Asian civilization specializing in Cavalry.

## Civ Bonuses

- Houses are not required to support population
- Start game with -100 Wood
- Cavalry Archers cost -25% in Castle Age/ -30% in Imperial Age
- Trebuchets are 35% more accurate.

## Team Bonus

Stables are 20% faster

## Unique Units

- **Tarkan** — Unique unit of the Huns

## Unique Techs

- **Atheism** (Castle Age) — See in-game tech tree
- **Imperial Age Unique Tech** (Imperial Age) — See in-game tech tree

## How to Play Huns

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Lean on the no-houses bonus to push a fast Scout opening, then transition smoothly into Cavalry Archers or Light Cavalry as you reach Castle Age — the discounted Cav Archers make that pivot especially cheap.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Field Heavy Cavalry Archers as your gold unit, back them with Hussar as the cheap trash frontline, and bring Trebuchets (boosted by the accuracy bonus) to break down buildings.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Mass Elite Tarkans to tear through fortifications, pair them with Heavy Cavalry Archers for ranged damage, and add Siege Rams to flatten anything left standing.
