Berbers are a Cavalry and Naval civilization from North Africa.

## How to Play Berbers

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Apply early Feudal pressure with a Scout Rush, leaning on the faster villagers to reach Feudal smoothly. Once you hit the Castle Age, the -20% stable discount makes cheap mounted units the backbone of your army — flood the map with discounted stable units, or branch into Camel Archers and Genitours depending on what your opponent fields.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Build around Camel Archers as your gold-based core, padded out with Hussars as the cheap trash unit to soak hits and raid, and add Bombard Cannons or Trebuchets to break buildings and siege lines.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Pack Camel Archers behind a wall of Heavy Scorpions to shred massed infantry and archers, with Bombard Cannons clearing fortifications and enemy siege as the ball rolls forward.

**Recommended build:** [Scout Rush](/en/builds/18pop-scouts)
