## How to Play Vikings

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Lean on the infantry HP bonus and open with a two-militia drush in Feudal to pressure your opponent's economy, then settle into a calm, defensive Feudal Age while you rush to Imperial. Vikings reward an aggressive early game and a fast push to Imp — their late game is weaker, so the plan is to close out games before the opponent stabilizes.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Pair Skirmishers as your trash core with Berserks as your gold-line punch, backed by Trebuchets (or Castle-dropped siege) for buildings. Note that Vikings lack Thumb Ring, so their ranged units are a support layer rather than the centerpiece.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Mass Berserks as the frontline, add Heavy Scorpions to shred clumped units and infantry, and bring Trebuchets to demolish buildings as you grind forward.
