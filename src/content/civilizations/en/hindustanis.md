Hindustanis are a South Asian civilization specializing in Gunpowder and Cavalry.

## Civ Bonuses

- Villagers cost -10% Dark, -15% Feudal, -20% Castle, -25% Imperial
- Fishermen work 15% faster and carry +15
- Camels +1/+1 armor

## Team Bonus
