Hindustanis are a South Asian civilization specializing in Gunpowder and Cavalry.

## Civ Bonuses

- Villagers cost -10% Dark, -15% Feudal, -20% Castle, -25% Imperial
- Fishermen work 15% faster and carry +15
- Camels +1/+1 armor

## Team Bonus

## How to Play Hindustanis

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Open with a flexible Scout Rush to apply early pressure and keep your options open. Transition based on what your opponent does: lean into archers when you need to punish forward buildings, or shift to camels and light cavalry to dominate the cavalry war and shut down enemy mounted units.

**Ideal late-game composition:** A realistic, sustainable mix pairs Hand Cannoneers for ranged damage with Hussar as your trash frontline, all backed by Bombard Cannons or Trebuchets for siege. Swapping in Imperial Camels with Skirmishers is a strong alternative when you face heavy cavalry.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Mass Imperial Camels as a durable, hard-hitting core, screen them with the 9-range Hand Cannoneers that Shatagni enables, and add Bombard Cannons to level buildings and out-range enemy siege.
