Bengalis are a South Asian civilization specializing in Elephant and Naval.

## Civ Bonuses

- Elephants resist 25% damage
- Ships +5 carry capacity and regenerate HP
- Farming does not require wheelbarrow or hand cart
- +2 Villagers at start

## Team Bonus

Monastery upgrades cost -50%

## Unique Units

- **Ratha** — Unique unit of the Bengalis

## Unique Techs

- **Paiks** (Castle Age) — Ratha and Elephant Archer attack 18% faster
- **Mahayana** (Imperial Age) — Villagers take up 0.5 less population
