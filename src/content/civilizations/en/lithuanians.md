Lithuanians are a Eastern European civilization specializing in Cavalry and Monk.

## Civ Bonuses

- Start with +150 food
- Spearman and Skirmisher lines cost -15% food
- Monks convert 20% faster for each Relic garrisoned in Monastery (max +60%)
- Cavalry +1 attack for each Relic garrisoned in Monastery (max +4)

## Team Bonus

Relic gold generation +100%

## Unique Units

- **Leitis** — Unique unit of the Lithuanians

## Unique Techs

- **Hill Forts** (Castle Age) — Town Centers +3 attack
- **Tower Shields** (Imperial Age) — Spearman line and Skirmishers +2 pierce armor
