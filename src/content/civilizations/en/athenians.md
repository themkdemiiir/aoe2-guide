Athenians are a Ancient Mediterranean civilization specializing in Naval and Archer.

## Civ Bonuses

- Ships +2 armor
- Docks work 20% faster
- Archers +1 attack
- Town Centers +5 LOS

## Team Bonus

Docks work 10% faster

## Unique Units

- **Hoplite** — Unique unit of the Athenians

## Unique Techs

- **Athenian Democracy** (Castle Age) — Archers +1 range
- **Themistocles** (Imperial Age) — War Galleys attack 15% faster
