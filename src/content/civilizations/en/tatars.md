Tatars are a Central Asian civilization specializing in Cavalry Archer.

## Civ Bonuses

- Herdables provide +50% food (sheep yield more)
- Cavalry Archers fire +1 additional projectile per attack in Imperial Age
- Cavalry units +2 line of sight on hills
- Free Parthian Tactics

## Team Bonus

Cavalry Archers +2 line of sight

## Unique Units

- **Keshik** — Unique unit of the Tatars

## Unique Techs

- **Silk Armor** (Castle Age) — Scout line, Cavalry Archers, and Steppe Lancers +1/+1 armor
- **Timurid Siegecraft** (Imperial Age) — Trebuchets have +2 range; Flaming Camels available
