Tatars are a Cavalry Archer civilization from Central Asia.

## How to Play Tatars

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Pressure with a Scout Rush in Feudal, then transition into Cavalry Archers in Castle Age — your free Thumb Ring makes them accurate immediately. Steppe Lancers, Camels, or Light Cavalry are flexible alternatives depending on what your opponent fields.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Heavy Cavalry Archers backed by Hussars (their armor benefits from your unique tech) and Trebuchets. A Keshik, Skirmisher, and Trebuchet mix is the strong second option when you need to grind out a longer game.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Long-range Trebuchets — 18 tiles with Siege Engineers plus your unique tech — paired with Heavy Cavalry Archers and Keshiks to shred buildings and armies from a safe distance.

**Recommended build:** [Scout Rush](/en/builds/18pop-scouts)
