Macedonians are a Ancient Mediterranean civilization specializing in Infantry.

## Civ Bonuses

- Infantry +10 HP
- Siege units -15% cost
- Scout line +2 LOS
- Monks require 4 units to convert

## Team Bonus

Infantry +1 pierce armor

## Unique Units

- **Phalangite** — Unique unit of the Macedonians

## Unique Techs

- **Sarissa** (Castle Age) — Spearman line +2 melee armor
- **Argyraspids** (Imperial Age) — Infantry attack 10% faster
