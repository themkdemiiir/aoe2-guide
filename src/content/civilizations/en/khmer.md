Khmer are a Southeast Asian civilization specializing in Siege and Elephant Civilzation.

## Civ Bonuses

- Prereq buildings aren't required to advance to further ages or unlock other buildings
- Battle Elephants are +15% faster
- Villagers can garrison in Houses

## Team Bonus

Scorpions have +1 range

## Unique Units

- **Ballista Elephant** — Unique unit of the Khmer

## Unique Techs

- **Tusk Swords** (Castle Age) — See in-game tech tree
- **Double Crossbow** (Imperial Age) — See in-game tech tree

## How to Play Khmer

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Lean on the building-prerequisite bonus to skip the barracks and fully wall your base early, while fast-farming farms keep your food count high. Open with Scouts behind that wall, then flow into Scouts, Knights, or Light Cavalry as you push into Castle Age.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Spam Hussar as your trash backbone, add a gold core of Heavy Scorpions or Ballista Elephants (Arbalest works as an alternative), and finish the mix with Trebuchets for siege.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Stack Heavy Scorpions with Ballista Elephants and back them with Trebuchets for a slow but devastating push.
