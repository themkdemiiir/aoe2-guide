Thracians are a Ancient Mediterranean civilization specializing in Infantry and Cavalry.

## Civ Bonuses

- Barracks units +1 melee armor
- Stable work 15% faster
- Farms cost -15%
- Infantry +10% speed

## Team Bonus

Barracks units +1 pierce armor

## Unique Units

- **Thracian Warrior** — Unique unit of the Thracians

## Unique Techs

- **Odrysian Tactics** (Castle Age) — Cavalry +1 attack
- **Thracian Shock** (Imperial Age) — Infantry +10 HP
