Japanese are an Infantry civilization from East Asia.

## How to Play Japanese

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Lean on the fast-attacking infantry bonus and open with a Man-at-Arms rush, then transition straight into an early Archery Range to keep the pressure on. Japanese fall off in the late game, so aim to convert this aggression into a decisive lead and close the match out before opponents stabilize.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Pair Halberdiers (your trash anti-cavalry line) with Arbalest as the gold-spending core, and back them up with Trebuchets. Yasama-boosted Towers give your defensive line extra reach, helping you hold positions while the Arbalest fire.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Mass Halberdier and Arbalest behind a wall of Onagers, with Trebuchets to crack buildings. The Siege Engineers bonus extends siege range, letting your Onagers and Trebs out-range enemy fortifications as the ball rolls forward.
