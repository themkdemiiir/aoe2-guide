Vietnamese are an Archer civilization from Southeast Asia.

## How to Play Vietnamese

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Lean on a Scouts opening that flows into Skirmishers, or run a Spearman-Skirmisher mix to apply early pressure cheaply. A Scouts-into-Cavalry-Archer line is also viable. Avoid committing to a one-range Archer opening — Vietnamese have no Camel to back up an Archer-heavy game, so the more flexible Scout-and-trash transitions serve you better.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Center your army on Rattan Archers (their pierce armor shrugs off enemy archer fire) supported by Halberdiers as your trash anti-cavalry, with Bombard Cannons for siege and demolition. If you need to economize on gold, Arbalests can stand in for some of the Rattan Archers.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Mass Rattan Archers as the ranged core, screen them with Battle Elephants as a durable frontline meatshield, and add Bombard Cannons to clear buildings and enemy siege as you push.

**Recommended build:** [Scouts into Skirmishers](/en/builds/scouts-into-skirms)
