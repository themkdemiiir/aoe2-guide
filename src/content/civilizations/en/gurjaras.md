Gurjaras are a South Asian civilization specializing in Cavalry and Naval.

## Civ Bonuses

- Start with 2 Camel Scouts
- Camels and Skirmishers counter cavalry effectively
- Mills produce unlimited food using livestock
- Chakram Throwers available at Archery Range (unique ranged infantry)

## Team Bonus

Camel and Battle Elephant units +1 Pierce Armor

## Unique Units

- **Shrivamsha Rider** — Unique unit of the Gurjaras

## Unique Techs

- **Kshatriyas** (Castle Age) — Military units cost -25% food
- **Frontier Guards** (Imperial Age) — Camel Riders and Elephant Archers +4 melee armor

## How to Play Gurjaras

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Open with single-range Archers in Feudal Age, then transition into Crossbowmen reinforced by Camel Riders once you reach Castle Age, leaning on the food discount from Kshatriyas to keep production flowing.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Build around Chakram Throwers as your gold-cost core, add Hussars as the trash backbone to soak melee and chase down stragglers, and round it out with Trebuchets or Bombard Cannons for siege.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Mass Chakram Throwers behind a wall of Heavy Camel Riders, with Bombard Cannons providing the long-range punch to break through fortified positions.
