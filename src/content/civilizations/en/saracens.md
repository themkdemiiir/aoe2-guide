Saracens are a Camel and Naval civilization from the Middle East.

## How to Play Saracens

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Begin with a single-range Archer opening in Feudal, then leverage the dirt-cheap 5% Market to fast-Castle. From there you have two strong directions — keep applying pressure with an Archer-and-Siege push, or transition into Crossbows backed by Camels to stay flexible against cavalry.

**Ideal late-game composition:** A practical Imperial army pairs Arbalests with Hussars for the trash backbone, though Heavy Camels with Skirmishers (or even a pure Mameluke mass) are viable swaps; round it out with Siege Onagers or Trebuchets for the siege punch.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Stack Mamelukes with Siege Onagers and Trebuchets, adding a few Hussars to deal with enemy Monks.

**Recommended build:** [19 Pop 1-Range Archers](/en/builds/19pop-archers)
