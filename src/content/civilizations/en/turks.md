Turks are a Gunpowder civilization from the Middle East.

## How to Play Turks

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Lean on a Scout opening that flexes into Light Cavalry, Knights, camels, or Cavalry Archers, or open with single-range Archers and ride the faster gold mining into an Archer plus Camel/Light Cavalry follow-up. Either path keeps you mobile while your gunpowder economy comes online.

**Ideal late-game composition:** Mass Heavy Cavalry Archers buffed by Zepaji's bonus HP, back them with the free upgraded Hussars (extra pierce armor) as your trash line, and add Bombard Cannons whose Artillery range bonus lets your siege out-position the enemy.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Roll forward with Heavy Cavalry Archers shielded by Hussars and supported by Bombard Cannons — a durable, hard-hitting ball that grinds through static defenses.

**Recommended build:** [28 Pop Turks Fast Imp](/en/builds/28pop-turks-fast-imp)
