Burgundians are a Cavalry civilization from Western Europe.

## How to Play Burgundians

**Ideal opening (Dark → Castle):** Burgundians have no real Feudal aggression, so play a quiet, defensive Feudal Age — wall up, take the cheap Bow Saw and Heavy Plow economy upgrades, and grow your economy rather than commit to a rush. Push to Castle Age and transition into Light Cavalry or Cavaliers, while using Monks to fight for Relics (your Knights can carry them).

**Ideal late-game composition:** Lean into the civilization's double-gold economy from Burgundian Vineyards and field Coustillier backed by Hand Cannoneers, then close games out with Flemish Revolution and Bombard Cannons once your gold supply allows it.

**Deathball composition (closed maps / team games):** Mass Coustillier and Hand Cannoneers with Bombard Cannon support — a gold-heavy ball that grinds through fortified positions on closed maps and in team games.
