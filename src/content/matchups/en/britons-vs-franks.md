Britons vs Franks is a classic open-map matchup pitting ranged power and economic boom against early cavalry pressure. Britons want to defend and outscale into a longbow-and-trebuchet late game; Franks want to deny the boom with knight raids and force the game to end before Britons reach their Imperial Age power spike.

## Feudal Age

Britons typically open Scouts or Archers, then transition into the 1-Range Archer build leveraging the cheap Town Center bonus for an early second TC. Franks usually skip Loom (free with their bonus) and run a fast Scout opening, then either continue cavalry pressure or commit to an early Castle Age Knight Rush.

Watch for: Franks scouts hitting your archer line while you're walling. Always wall first, then archer.

## Castle Age

This is where the matchup pivots. Britons' Crossbowmen with Yeomen reach a tier of range no other archer civ matches; Franks' Knights with +20% HP (their civ bonus) and cheap Castle Age age-up (free Farm upgrades) are the strongest raw cavalry on the field. The Britons player wants to stay back, mass Crossbows, and add Pikemen for spear support. The Franks player wants to flood Knights, especially against an under-walled position.

Decisive moments:
- **Britons** must research Ballistics and add 3+ Archery Ranges before Franks' second wave of Knights arrives.
- **Franks** must research Forging + Iron Casting and have at least 8–10 Knights pushing simultaneously, not in dribs and drabs. (Note: Franks do NOT have access to Bloodlines — their cavalry HP bonus partially compensates.)

If Britons stabilizes Castle Age safely, they win the long game. If Franks lands a Castle Age timing attack with raids, the game often ends before Imperial.

## Imperial Age

In a long game, Britons win. Arbalests with Yeomen and Thumb Ring shred Cavaliers and Paladins from range no Frank can close. Trebuchets with Warwolf finish the game. Franks' answer is to commit to Throwing Axemen + Paladins + a second front (a flanking castle or boomed second base), but it's an uphill battle vs a settled Britons opponent.

## Summary

- **Open map (Arabia, Hideout):** slight edge to whichever player plays their power spike better. Skill match.
- **Closed map (Arena, Black Forest):** favors Britons — they reach Imperial Age with full Arbalest upgrades, Franks need to commit to Castle drops or accept the loss of map control.
- **Water map:** doesn't really matter — both civs have mediocre navies.

Overall: roughly even at high skill, slight Britons favor in extended games.
