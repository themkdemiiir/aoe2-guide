You don't have to reinvent the wheel to improve at Age of Empires II — the community has produced excellent, well-tuned material. These are the resources we lean on, and that informed the guides on this site.

## Build orders

- **[AoE Companion](https://aoecompanion.com/build-guides)** — clean, categorised build guides (Scout Rush, Archer Rush, Fast Castle variants, drush, water). Our build orders are cross-checked against these.
- **[Build Order Guide](https://www.buildorderguide.com/)** — an interactive build-order tool you can step through.

## Unit counters & stats

- **[aoe2techtree.net](https://aoe2techtree.net)** — the full tech tree with *hidden* unit stats and attack bonuses. Indispensable when you want exact numbers.
- **Spirit of the Law (YouTube)** — deeply researched balance breakdowns, a beginner counters guide, and a "countering every unique unit" series.

## Strategy & improvement

- **[r/aoe2 wiki](https://www.reddit.com/r/aoe2/wiki/index/)** — a hub linking community guides, counter cheat-sheets, and beginner material.
- **T90Official, ViBE (YouTube)** — pro and educational creators whose build guides and coaching content are gold for ladder players.

## How to actually use them

Pick **one** weakness at a time — idle Town Center time, a shaky build order, poor scouting — fix it across a few games, then move to the next. Reviewing your own recorded games against these references is the fastest way to find the gap between what you *think* you did and what actually happened.

For a structured path, start with our [Learn](/en/learn/) chapters and the [Counters](/en/counters/) page.
