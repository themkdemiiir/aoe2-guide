Fortress is a closed map similar to Arena but with pre-built towers along the stone wall ring — giving each player stronger starting defenses. The heavier fortification makes Fast Castle near-universal; players race to Castle Age, boom their economy, then assault with siege. Unique units, siege, and monk-and-siege armies dominate. Relics outside the walls are the first contested resource.

## Opening

Standard opener is a 23-villager Fast Castle — identical to Arena. No Feudal military is produced since walls and towers protect against early aggression. Send the scout out the Fortress gate immediately to capture relics outside. First player to scout relics and send Monks wins the gold war. A 2nd Town Center is typically the first Castle Age build.

## Mid game

Siege production is critical. Trebuchets and Rams are needed to breach enemy Fortress walls, which have higher HP than Arena's plain walls due to the towers. Unique units perform well — Teutonic Knights, Janissaries, Huskarls, and Coustilliers all excel. Monks + Siege is the go-to combination. Bohemians, Turks, and Spanish shine especially in Imperial Age with gunpowder.

## Watch out for

- **Pre-built tower blind spots** — starting towers create dead zones where enemies can sneak a Ram without entering tower range.
- **Forgetting Masonry + Architecture** — walls and towers fall quickly without these upgrades; research them early in Castle Age.
- **False sense of security** — a well-timed forward Castle near your wall can collapse the position even on Fortress.
- **Stone drain** — Trebuchets and Castle drops consume stone rapidly; manage your stone economy carefully.
