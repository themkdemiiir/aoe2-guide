Steppe is a fully open map with almost no trees near starting positions. Resources are spread with minimal cover; walling is slow and expensive. Cavalry civs dominate completely — the open terrain lets Scouts and Knights raid from any angle without terrain obstruction. Civs that rely on walling or terrain defense are severely disadvantaged.

## Opening

The 18-pop Scout Rush is the standard opener. Virtually any fast Feudal works since walling is impractical — many players skip walls entirely and invest directly in military production. Civs with inherent speed bonuses (Cumans, Mongols, Berbers, Huns) cover the full map rapidly before the opponent can react.

## Mid game

Castle Age is a constant cavalry raid war. Relics scattered across the map are heavily contested; mounted Monks and fast cavalry fight for relic positions throughout Castle Age. Siege is difficult to defend since cavalry can circumvent it from any direction. Camel civs are notably disadvantaged — their primary counter unit doesn't translate to the cavalry-centric meta.

## Watch out for

- **Exposed economy** — assume your economy will be raided. Garrison sheep under TC early; prioritize Bloodlines and Scout production even in non-cavalry civs.
- **Relic neglect** — with 5 relics available and cavalry to grab them, neglecting relics loses the economic game rapidly.
- **Siege positioning** — Scorpions and Mangonels die instantly to 4+ cavalry; they need heavy escort or will not survive.
