## Chapter outline (author's ordering = most→least important)

1. **Foundation**
   1.1 Hotkeys — select-all production buildings (TC/rax/range/stable/SW/castle/dock),
       building go-to hotkeys (LC/mill/mining-camp/blacksmith/market/university),
       idle-vill hotkey spam. Rule: never click bottom-left UI; never click buildings; never minimap-jump between TC and army.
   1.2 Controls — gather points (tree/boar via set-gather-point; market→market auto-trade;
       trebs/rams/petards auto-deploy on buildings), patrol/attack-move, **shift-queue** (vill pathing,
       build-then-gather, resource chaining), attack-ground (siege; mango on 3x3 to avoid self-damage),
       stances (aggressive/defensive/stand-ground/no-attack), "stop" micro, unbind town bell (manual garrison instead),
       ctrl-drag = military only, alt-drag = civilians only.
   1.3 Creating villagers — NEVER idle TC. 25s idle = 1 vil = compounding ~1000 res lost over a long game.
   1.4 Build orders — don't reinvent; learn set builds. Open maps → scouts/archers for beginners;
       closed → FC variants (xbow/monks/knights/boom). Best for LEARNING: maa/drush on Arabia.
       1.4.1 Walling & placement — don't wall too early (hurts uptime); use rax/range/stable as wall;
       farms near mill/TC (mill by 3rd row); LC touch max trees; mining-camp 1-tile gap; resource/small/big walls.
   1.5 Scouting — manually scout (don't auto-scout); find 2x2 sheep, 2 boars, woodlines, golds, stone, berries, deer;
       scout enemy eco + upgrades (+1/+2 numbers on units); don't run scout into enemy TC (orbit pattern 1-2-3).

2. **Core fundamentals**
   2.1 Economy upgrades — double-bit axe ≈ +2 vils worth; LC upgrades top priority; farm upgrades = wood-saving;
       wheelbarrow worth it ~14-16 farms; handcart usually delayed to imp (500 res ≈ 1/3 of imp).
   2.2 Economy balance — minimize excess/float; float food slowly for age-ups; market loses ~30%; don't bank 1000 wood.
   2.3 Continuous production — never idle rax/range/stable; ~3.5 vils on gold per range of archers.
       Tool: aoe2-de-tools.herokuapp.com/villagers-required
   2.4 Lanchester's law — numbers compound (square law); 10v11 → snowballs to large leads; idling 2-3 units matters.
   2.5 Using your army — never fight a losing fight; high ground +25%/-25%; force enemy off hills by poking eco.
       2.5.1 Counter elaboration — counter isn't a counter if it can't kill / takes too long / loses to micro;
       xbow+bodkin powerspike unbeatable by feudal units; reaching castle first = huge.

3. **Common edges**
   3.1 Military upgrades — fletching/bodkin huge for archers (17 xbow vs 50 archer to volley a knight);
       +armor+bloodlines doubles xbows needed; feudal melee/skirm want armor first, ranged want damage first;
       bloodlines on scouts only worth ~6-7+ scouts.
   3.2 Counter units & transitions — transitions cost eco rebalance + uptime; don't stop old unit until new techs done; ~100 vils for full comp swap.
   3.3 Uptimes — 22 pop feudal min 10:05 (each vil/loom = 25s); 3TC arbalest <32 min (26-28 fastest);
       scout-knight imp ~32-36 min.
