## Evaluating a counter (4 factors)
1. Cost (expensive beating cheap ≠ a true counter)
2. Bonus damage (see aoe2techtree.net for hard numbers)
3. Armour & Pierce Armour (high PA needed to counter ranged)
4. Speed (no good if the target outruns the counter)

## Feudal Age "counter-square"
Units: Militia/MAA, Spearman, Archer, Skirmisher, Scout Cavalry (non-meso), Eagle Scout (meso).
- **Archer** beats Skirmisher? No — **Skirmisher beats Archer** (bonus dmg + 3 PA reduces archer to 1 dmg).
- **Scout Cavalry** beats Skirmisher (2 PA → skirm deals 1 dmg; fast enough to chase).
- **Spearman** beats Scout Cavalry (attack bonus vs cavalry) — hardest counter in feudal, but slow & fragile vs everything else.
- Skirmisher also has bonus vs Spearman (don't use spears vs skirms).
- **Man-at-Arms** strong vs most feudal units, weak to hit-and-run archers; safe under a tower.
- **Towers**: no murder holes can't hit adjacent units; villagers have big bonus vs stone defences (fastest way down); or build a counter-tower.
- **Eagle Scout** (meso): like scout, -1 atk, no cavalry weakness; MAA has small bonus but is slower/pricier → eagles lack a clean feudal counter.

## Castle Age (no uniques)
Units: Crossbow, Elite Skirm, Cavalry Archer, Long Swordsman, Pikeman, Eagle Warrior, Light Cav, Knight, Camel,
Battle Elephant, Monk, Petard, Battering Ram, Mangonel, Scorpion.
- **Knight** = most dominant castle-age unit (HP/atk/armor/speed) but ~2x cost. If you reach castle first, a few knights wreck a feudal army.
  - Archers+fletching deal 2 to +1-armor knight → 50 shots; scouts 3 dmg vs knight's 10; spears need numbers.
  - If you're feudal vs their castle: rush to castle ASAP.
- **Crossbowman** = 2nd most used (cheap upgrade from feudal archers). Xbow vs Knight is the key castle-age matchup.
- General: feudal counters carry up; add Pikeman (vs cav), Skirm (vs archers), Mangonel (vs massed foot), Camel (vs cav, anti-knight).
